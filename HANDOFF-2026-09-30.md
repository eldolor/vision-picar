## 7. Things learned the hard way

* **Verify at the source, not the listing.** Three decisive readings were
  wrong at page level: the ROSOrin's "4 encoders" (never reported to the
  host), "sold with an Orin Super fitted" (can't sustain 25 W), the Rover's
  "12.6 V / 2 A" (the charger). Read the vendor's driver or firmware, and
  get power claims in writing.
* **Closed loop is not "reports to the host".** A board can use its
  encoders internally and send nothing up the cable.
* **Check the manufacturer part number, not the title** -- Micro Center's
  "ROSOrin" was the Starter tier (mecanum only).
* **Micro Center and RobotShop block automated fetches** (Cloudflare 403);
  ask the user for a PDF or use their browser.
* **Never `git show X > file` to restore an untracked file**: the redirect
  empties it before `git show` fails. It cost two files once.
* **Other sessions commit to `dev` in parallel.** Work in a worktree and
  merge.
