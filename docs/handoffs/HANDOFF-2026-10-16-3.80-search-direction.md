## Why criterion 2 failed (the problem 3.80.F solves)

Pointing at a target and searching for one are different jobs. YOLOE +
CLIP and Qwen only point at a target in view. Today the cloud's
FORWARD/LEFT/RIGHT is the search; with the local tier answering instead,
nothing chooses where to go, and the policy turns in place to the time
limit (19 of 21). The cloud's driving brought the target into view in 4
houses, the spin in 2.

A second, separate effect: the simulator draws the target as an orange
rectangle captioned "red backpack". Qwen is a coin flip on it (6/12 yes,
`render-check.json`); the cloud reads the caption. So in simulation the
local arm's arrival can be refused where the cloud's is not (val 5). Decide
how criterion 2's re-run treats this BEFORE running it (e.g. the found
count of missions whose arrival reached the identity check, or a renderer
change) and write it down as an amendment.
