# Handoff 2026-10-15: 3.82 A refused goal must not lift a Stop's hold

Section: [3.82-refused-goal-hold.md](../plans/ros-alignment/3.82-refused-goal-hold.md).
Branch `plan/3.82-refused-goal-hold`. Not pushed: needs the user's go-ahead.

## What changed

* **`robot/server.py` `world_goal_set`.** A goal supersedes a Stop's
  goal-ending loop (bumps `goal_stop["gen"]`, clears the hold) only when the
  bridge took it AND no goal-ending stop was counted since the request
  arrived (`goal_stop["stops"]`, read first in the handler). A refused goal
  changes nothing. A goal a stop overtook while it waited for
  `goal_stop_lock` is answered `{"accepted": false, "reason": "stopped"}`
  and never sent; one the bridge took while a stop was pressed answers the
  same and the stop's loop ends it. A person's goal takes the driver slot at
  arbitration, holds it however long the bridge takes
  (`verb_running(person, restamp=...)`), re-stamps it only if accepted, and
  otherwise gives it back if nobody has driven since.
* **`world/bridge_faults.py` (new).** One rule for "can waiting mend a
  bridge error": transport/protocol errors, 5xx, 408, 409, 429 may pass;
  other 4xx, a non-JSON 200, a URL with no scheme or a redirect loop may
  not. Carried as fields on `POST /world/goal` (`bridge_status`,
  `retryable`), headers on GET/DELETE (`x-bridge-retryable`,
  `x-bridge-status`) and on `RosWorld.get_map()`'s unusable map.
* **Explore** ends the mission `failed` on a non-retryable goal refusal, map
  or goal read, naming the status and what to check (`hint()`); a transient
  fault still waits. `RemoteNavigator.available()` names the fault at start
  (was "needs nav2").

## Numbers

* Live (`evaluations/refused-goal-382/`): refused goal right after a Stop
  whose cancels fail: **0.0 cm** true displacement over 8 s on the fix,
  **128 cm** on `dev` (goal A still active). Accepted goal after a Stop:
  `succeeded` in 4.7 s.
* 35 tests in `tests/test_refused_goal_hold.py`, each red first on `dev` or
  on the review round it answers.
* Eight review rounds (both reviewers each). Rounds 1-6 each found a real
  bug, three of them introduced by the previous round's fix; rounds 7-8
  found nothing above low.

## Open, for the user

* Any accepted goal -- explore's, a script's -- supersedes a person's Stop
  loop, not only a person's re-sent goal (as on `dev`; the twin's Stop also
  ends the brain's mission).
* Lost reply: the bridge takes a goal but the reply times out, so the route
  answers `ros_unavailable`; nav2 drives a goal the caller was told failed
  (as on `dev`, with or without a Stop).
* A verb under `drive: ros` clears the hold without `goal_gen_lock`
  (3.76 A's code, untouched here).
* A secret with surrounding whitespace breaks every client alike; it reads
  as a transient fault to the bridge clients.
* Policies other than explore still wait on a non-retryable map (the field
  is there for them to read).
* Twin toast says "Could not send the goal" for the taken-then-stopped
  case (cosmetic; a twin change needs a deploy).
* Under `drive: direct` with a ROS world, a person's accepted goal's own
  `ros` wheel commands are refused for 1 s after the accept (sim-only).
