# Handoff 2026-10-15: 3.84 A person's Stop is the last word over autonomy

Section: [3.84-person-stop-last-word.md](../plans/ros-alignment/3.84-person-stop-last-word.md).
Branch `plan/3.84-person-stop-last-word`. Not pushed: needs the user's go-ahead.

## What changed

* **A person's Stop latches autonomy off** (`robot/server.py`). A `/stop`
  from a driver ranked as a person (`twin-dpad`, `teleop-operator`, unnamed)
  sets `goal_stop["autonomy_stopped"]` before its `robot.stop()`. While set,
  every `brain`/`teleop` `/action`, direct `/wheels` and `brain`-named goal is
  refused `person_stop`, at arrival and again at execution. The runner turns
  that into `StoppedByPerson` and ends the mission `cancelled`,
  `cancelled_by: "stop"` (the twin already words it "Cancelled -- Stop
  pressed"). Only `POST /autonomy/resume`, sent by the brain at the START of
  a mission, lifts it. A person's own verbs and goals are never refused;
  unnamed goals keep 3.23/3.82's behaviour. Explore names itself on its goals
  (`RemoteNavigator`, `x-driver: brain`).
* **A verb's hold lift is locked and stop-aware** (`robot/server.py`). Under
  `drive: ros` a verb lifts the Stop's goal hold under `goal_gen_lock`, only
  if no stop was counted since it arrived (otherwise it is refused
  `stopped`), and the hold returns when the last verb lifted under that stop
  ends while the Stop's loop is still ending a goal.
* **A verb's stop baseline is the server's** (`robot/interface.py`
  `VERB_STOP_BASELINE`, `carry_out_verb`, `SafetyController.run_verb`,
  `RosDriveRobot._begin`): a Stop that lands between the server's last latch
  read and the verb's first motion ends it unmoved.
* `/autonomy/resume`'s ALB path patterns in the kept ECS templates (the
  route guards).

## Numbers

* In process (`tests/test_person_stop.py`, 20 tests, 27 cases, each bug test
  red first on `dev` `fbb1b0b` or on the review round it answers): a D-pad,
  unnamed or `teleop-operator` Stop during explore: nav2 0.0 rad/s over 3 s
  (`dev` 3.0), no explore goal reaches the bridge, mission `cancelled`/`stop`.
  A verb arbitrated before a Stop no longer lifts its hold (`dev` 3.0 rad/s);
  a person's verb during the Stop's loop turns > 30 deg and nav2 stays at 0.0
  after it (`dev` 3.0). A brain turn whose latch read preceded a Stop: 0 deg
  (round 2: 60 deg, direct and ROS).
* Full gate (`ready --full`, `0717cb1`): 2850 passed, 0 failed.
* Live (`evaluations/person-stop-384/`, `scaled_house`, the real robot
  server, brain and ROS container): a D-pad Stop while explore drove at
  8.5 cm/s -- **0.0 cm** true displacement over 10 s and the mission
  `cancelled`/`stop` in 0.34 s; on `dev` the robot drove **29.8 cm** and the
  mission kept running. A D-pad move after the Stop and a new mission both
  drive.
