## Where it stands

* **Built:** `SIM_MAP=procthor:val:<0-999>` loads any of the 1,000 houses
  of ProcTHOR-10K's `val` split (Apache-2.0), rasterised into the 0.30 m
  grid: walls, door gaps, the front door shut, floor furniture (bottom
  below 0.25 m), tables as four legs, a red backpack placed by a seed from
  the house index. `tools/procthor_import.py` fetches the pinned, hashed
  sources once; the suite reads `sim/maps/procthor_data/val.json.gz` and
  never touches the network.
* **Met:** topology 1000/1000, outline 97.4%, furniture 99.85%, usable
  570/570 (houses with >= 3 rooms), sweep ends cleanly 100/100, build 6 ms
  median, one live mission through the brain (`max_steps`).
* **Failed and pinned:** criterion 5b, found 84 of the first 100 usable
  houses (bar 90). The sweep test pins 84; the bar stays at 90.
