# Handoff 2026-10-09: 3.48 -- a local identity check (measurement only)

**Branch** `plan/3.48-local-identity` (continued on a local branch from its
`2bb9cb0`). Section: `docs/plans/ros-alignment/3.48-local-identity.md`
(criteria committed before any scoring; amendments 1-3 each committed
before the runs they affect; the tables; the caveats).

**Result.** Two rounds.

1. **A10G rescoring, bf16:** Qwen2.5-VL-3B met criteria 1-4 (precision
   97.4%, recall 51.4%, 0 of 34 YES on the teal bin the cloud confabulated)
   and failed 6 by arithmetic (7.51 GB of weights).
2. **On the Jetson at 15 W, llama.cpp GGUFs, five models** (the user asked
   for NVIDIA Nemotron "and a few more"): **Qwen2.5-VL-3B Q4_K_M** met 1-4
   again (precision 97.1%, recall 89.2%, 0/34 teal bin, against Opus's
   67.9% precision) and failed 5 (p90 5.58 s for the full reply; 2.84 s to
   the deciding token) and 6 (3.48 GB, bar 3.25). Qwen3-VL-2B,
   Cosmos-Reason2-2B (NVIDIA) and LFM2-VL-1.6B say yes to the teal bin on
   33-34 of 34 frames; SmolVLM2 misses most targets (41.9%). No Nemotron VL
   fits the board (the smallest with vision is 8B, 7.37 GB at 4 bits).

**No fallback is recommended as measured.** The next step worth taking is a
follow-up section for Qwen2.5-VL-3B Q4 with generation stopped at the
decision token and memory measured alongside the running stack, criteria
fixed before it runs.

**Lessons recorded in the amendments.** The first measured runs were void:
llama-server's default 8 GiB host-RAM prompt cache filled the Jetson's
unified memory until the OOM killer took the server. Run it with
`--cache-ram 0`. And a one-walk smoke test caught the scorer reading a
whitespace token as the decision.
