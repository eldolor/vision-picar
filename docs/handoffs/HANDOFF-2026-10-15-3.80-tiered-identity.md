## What is built

With `tier_local_vlm_url` / `LOCAL_VLM_URL` set, the tiered policy puts every
question a frame raises to the local VLM first: today's four triggers, and
`uncertain_candidate` (a candidate below perception's gate with CLIP P >=
`tier_local_band_low`, 0.5). The cloud is asked only after two unable answers
to that question, or when the local tier is absent. Off by default: with no
URL the per-frame output is byte-identical to `dev`.

* `brain/tiered.py`: `_Question(trigger, episode, unable)`; one waiting slot
  (`_waiting`); band and detection episodes (`LOCAL_RESET_FRAMES` tolerance);
  the frames' calls share 3.80.C's one local slot with the arrival.
* `brain/perceive.py`: `Perception.strongest()`, `Perception.promoted()`.
* Config: `tier_local_band_low` (`config/robot.yaml`, `control/brain_config.py`,
  `control/brain_server.py`).
* Specs: `docs/engineering/policy/ENGINEERING.md` ("The frames' questions
  through the local tier"), perception engineering, both ARCHITECTURE files.
