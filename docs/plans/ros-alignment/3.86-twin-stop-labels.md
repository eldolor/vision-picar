# 3.86 The twin names a Stop's refusals

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.86; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.82 and 3.84 added two refusal reasons the robot server answers:
`stopped` (a press, or a goal, that a Stop overtook after it was sent: not
carried out) and `person_stop` (the brain, refused because a person pressed
Stop; [3.84-person-stop-last-word.md](3.84-person-stop-last-word.md), round 2
#6, open for the user). The twin's `REFUSAL_LABEL` (`web-twin/app.js`) knows
neither, so the history log tags both `[VETOED]`, the fallback for a server
that sends no reason, and the Sim tab's Safety readout flashes `VETOED`: a
press a person's own Stop overtook reads as a safety veto. The user approved
the fix and its deploy (2026-10-16).

**Design.** Two labels: `stopped` -> `STOPPED` ("a stop overtook it"),
`person_stop` -> `STOPPED BY A PERSON`. The Safety readout's `VETOED` flash
is for a safety veto, so neither reason flashes it. The detail text the
server sends is shown as today. **Widened by review round 1:** the flash is
turned ON for a veto (`safety_distance`, or no reason) instead of off for
two exceptions, so a preemption or a fault never flashes it either; labels
for `no_feedback` and `ros_unavailable`; an unknown reason is shown by its
own name, never VETOED.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Red first.** Playwright, 390 px phone viewport (`tests/test_ui.py`'s
   pattern): (a) a D-pad press answered `{executed: false, reason:
   "stopped"}` logs `[STOPPED]`, never `VETOED` or `SAFETY VETO`, and the
   Safety readout never shows `VETOED` within 1 s; (b) `/health`'s
   `last_refusal` with reason `person_stop` shows `STOPPED BY A PERSON` in the
   refusal readout. Both fail on `dev` (`48b5cd2`) and pass with the fix; a
   `safety_distance` press still flashes `VETOED` (a guard, passes on both).
2. **Evidence:** a phone-size (390 px) screenshot of the Sim tab's log after a
   `stopped` press.
3. **Deployed:** `service/static/sync.sh` to the twin's bucket and
   distribution, the invalidation `Completed`, and the deployed `app.js`
   byte-identical to `git show HEAD:web-twin/app.js`.
