# 3.56 The operator sees the cloud: reachable status, and a shorter wait at arrival

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.56; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** Two items from 3.49 (offline is a mode), approved by the user on
2026-10-09:

* **(a) `cloud_reachable`.** Nothing tells the operator the cloud is down
  until a mission parks `arrived_unconfirmed` (3.47). The brain should say
  so while the mission runs, and the twin should show it: "cloud:
  reachable", "unreachable since 14:02:11", or "unknown" before the first
  look.
* **(b) `arrival_confirm_timeout_s`, default 8 s (the user's choice, "~8
  s").** The arrival confirmation shares B3.2's 20 s vision timeout, and
  the budget is three failures, so a cloud that HANGS (rather than refusing)
  keeps a robot parked at its target for about a minute before it ends
  `arrived_unconfirmed`. Its own shorter timeout should park it in about
  25 s.

That margin is positive but thin, and 98 calls from two evenings is a small
sample; a cold Lambda or Bedrock throttling could exceed it. What a too-short
timeout costs is bounded: the call is counted as the cloud's failure (3.47),
retried within B3.2's budget, and at worst the mission parks
`arrived_unconfirmed` and 3.53 asks again later. It can never produce a
wrong `found`. So 8 s is kept as the user chose; criterion 8 below pins
that the slowest recorded latency still confirms.

## Design

**(a)** One `CloudWatch` per brain process (`control/reconfirm.py`, beside
3.53's `Reconfirmer`, reusing its `health_probe()`: the vision service's
free `GET /health`, never a paid `/navigate`).
* States: `unknown` (no probe yet), `reachable` (the last probe answered
  200), `unreachable` (refused, timed out, or not 200). `since` is when the
  current state began; repeated results do not move it.
* Its thread probes every `cloud_probe_s` (default 15 s, 0 = off) **only
  while a mission of a cloud policy (`vision`, `tiered`) is running**.
  Otherwise it sleeps and probes nothing.
* **One prober at a time:** after an `arrived_unconfirmed` ending no
  mission is running, so the watch is idle and 3.53's late check probes;
  its probes are recorded into the same watch, so the status keeps moving.
* It never touches the robot and never feeds B3.2's budget; it is a
  description, not a failsafe. Reported in `GET /mission/status` as
  `cloud`, not in `/health` (M5: a cloud outage is not something a release
  can be blamed for).
* "Reachable" means the service answers `/health`; Bedrock behind it can
  still fail (3.53 found `/health` answers through a Bedrock outage).

**(b)** `MissionRunner(arrival_confirm_timeout_s=...)` bounds the arrival
confirmation instead of `vision_timeout_s`.
* The confirmation's own HTTP call uses the same deadline: the brain builds
  a second cloud client for confirmations (`vision_fn_for(...,
  timeout_s=arrival_confirm_timeout_s)`), passed to `TieredVision` as
  `confirm_vision_fn`. A hang is then ended by httpx (`CloudUnavailable`,
  so 3.47 attributes it to the cloud), not by abandoning a thread.
* The runner's guard sits a grace above it (`+2 s`), as a backstop; its
  synthetic `CloudUnavailable` (3.47) stays.
* **One call in flight:** if a previous confirmation's thread is still
  alive (the backstop fired), the next confirmation does not start another
  call; it fails at once as the cloud's.
* `config/robot.yaml` and `control/brain_config.py` gain both keys
  together; `arrival_confirm_timeout_s` must be positive and (superseded by
  amendment 2: clamped to, not refused above) at most
  `vision_timeout_s`.

**Interactions checked:** 3.47's attribution (a confirmation timeout is
`CloudUnavailable` either way); 3.49's offline tests (in-process missions,
no brain server, so no watch); 3.53's late check (its `late_ask()` keeps the
B3.2 timeout: it is one paid call with no robot waiting).

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Honest states.** Before the first probe `cloud.state` is `unknown`;
   after a 200 it is `reachable`; after a refused or timed-out probe or a
   non-200 it is `unreachable`. `since` changes only on a transition.
2. **Free and bounded.** Over a running cloud-policy mission of duration T,
   the watch makes at most `T / cloud_probe_s + 1` probes, all to
   `/health`, and 0 `/navigate` calls. With no cloud-policy mission
   running (idle, `frontier`, `explore`), it makes 0 probes.
3. **Never the robot, never B3.2.** A tiered mission with `/health` down
   and `/navigate` up ends `found` with `vision_failures` 0, the same steps
   and the same robot calls as with the watch off (progress pair: same
   outcome and steps).
4. **One prober at a time.** During 3.53's late wait the watch's own
   thread makes 0 probes; the late check's probes are recorded, so
   `cloud.state` turns `reachable` when the cloud returns.
5. **The twin at 390 px.** The Remote brain panel shows "Cloud: unknown",
   "reachable" (safe style) or "unreachable since HH:MM:SS" (warn style).
   A UI test that fails without the change, run in module order; a phone
   screenshot.
6. **The default sits above the data.** `arrival_confirm_timeout_s` (8 s)
   is above the slowest recorded cloud call (6.78 s of 98). Met by the
   table above; pinned by a test that reads the default.
7. **A hanging cloud parks sooner.** With `/navigate` hanging, the parked
   wait (first arrival confirmation to the `arrived_unconfirmed` end) is at
   most `3 x (arrival_confirm_timeout_s + 2 s) + 1 s` and less than the
   same mission with the confirmation on `vision_timeout_s`. Measured
   scaled down in process (0.8 s against 2.0 s), and once live at the
   defaults (bar: 31 s).
8. **Slow but alive still confirms.** A cloud answering every call in
   6.78 s (the slowest recorded) ends `found` with exactly 1 arrival
   confirmation on all 12 clear starts (scaled: 0.68 s against a 0.8 s
   timeout).
9. **One call in flight.** In criterion 7's hang run, peak concurrent
   confirmation calls to the cloud is 1.
10. **Config moves together.** `load_brain_config()` accepts the new keys
    from `config/robot.yaml`, rejects `arrival_confirm_timeout_s <= 0` or
    above `vision_timeout_s`, and rejects a `cloud_probe_s` that is
    negative.
11. **Live.** Robot and brain on this section's ports, `VISION_URL` a dead
    port: within one probe interval of a tiered start `cloud.state` reads
    `unreachable`. Then a stand-in vision service comes up on that port:
    within one interval it reads `reachable`. The robot's pose is the same
    with the watch running and stopped.
12. **No regression.** 3.47, 3.49 and 3.53's tests unchanged and green;
    `ready` passes.

**Amendment 1 (2026-10-09, before any measured run).** (i) The runner's
guard above the confirmation's deadline is a quarter of the deadline, at
most 2 s (2 s at the 8 s default; 0.2 s at the scaled 0.8 s), not a flat
2 s; criterion 7's bar is unchanged and so is looser than the guard at
scale. (ii) Criterion 8's scaled run slows only the arrival confirmation
(0.68 s, every one); the trigger calls answer at once, so twelve missions do
not add about 50 s of trigger waits to the fast tier. The confirmation is
the only call this section's deadline governs. (iii) One existing test was
updated for the new keyword: `tests/test_brain_server.py`'s stand-in for
`_tiered_vision_fn` now accepts `confirm_vision_fn`.

**Amendment 2 (2026-10-09, after `/code-review` on `6c9934d`, before the
runs it affects).** Criterion 10's "rejects `arrival_confirm_timeout_s`
above `vision_timeout_s`" is withdrawn: the shipped `config/robot.yaml`
sets the key, so any such refusal makes a deployment that lowers only
`vision_timeout_s` fail at load -- the walks Lambda's cold start. The
deadline is clamped to `vision_timeout_s` instead (it already was, in the
runner), and criterion 10 now reads: `load_brain_config()` accepts the new
keys, rejects `arrival_confirm_timeout_s <= 0` and a negative
`cloud_probe_s`, and loads the SHIPPED yaml with only `vision_timeout_s`
lowered, the runner clamping the confirmation to it. Added as criterion
13: **the arrival tick stays bounded** -- the whole confirmation step
(waiting for an async trigger still out, for a confirmation still out, and
the call) finishes within `vision_timeout_s` plus 0.5 s, as before 3.56, so
B3.3's tick deadline never aborts a mission that B3.2 should have counted.

**Self-review (Thermos rubric applied by hand -- this section ran as a
worker fork that cannot start a separate reviewer) on `3062488`:**

* A deployment that lowers only `vision_timeout_s` would have failed to
  load (the walks Lambda reads this file at cold start). Now only an
  explicitly set `arrival_confirm_timeout_s` above it is refused; the
  default is clamped. `test_10_lowering_only_the_vision_timeout_still_loads_and_clamps`.
* The in-flight backstop failed the next confirmation on the next tick
  (0.25 s), spending B3.2's budget at the tick's pace. Now it waits up to
  one guard for the call still out. `test_9_the_backstop_waits_for_the_call_in_flight_before_giving_up`.
* Recorded, not fixed: httpx's deadline is per phase, so a slow connect
  plus a slow read can outlast the guard; the backstop then holds "one call
  in flight".

**`/code-review` (medium, tests included) on `3062488`: three findings,
all verified and fixed, each pinned by a test that fails on `3062488`:**

1. Under the shipped async tier, the confirmation's 10 s guard also covered
   `confirm_arrival()`'s unbounded wait for a 20 s trigger call still out,
   so a slow but healthy cloud read as unreachable. The runner now drains
   it first with `TieredVision.wait_inflight()` on B3.2's timeout.
2. 3.53's `late_ask()` ignored a confirmation still in flight and could
   send a second paid call beside it. It now waits, then raises
   `TimeoutError` with nothing sent.
3. The confirmation's own client never received the searched rooms its
   `/navigate` call used to carry; `set_searched_rooms()` now reaches both.

**`/code-review` (medium) on `6c9934d`: three findings, all verified and
fixed (amendment 2 written first):**

1. The shipped yaml sets `arrival_confirm_timeout_s`, so the
   explicit-key refusal still broke a deployment that lowers only
   `vision_timeout_s`; the self-review's test passed on a stripped file.
   Now clamped, never refused; the test loads the shipped yaml.
2. The in-flight wait (20 s), the backstop join (10 s) and the call (10 s)
   could together outlast B3.3's 30 s tick deadline, so a mission B3.2
   should have counted was aborted as a hung loop. Now one deadline,
   `vision_timeout_s`, covers the whole step (criterion 13).
3. With the call cap spent, the runner waited for a slow trigger before the
   policy's free local refusal. `wait_inflight()` now returns at once when
   the cap is spent.

**`/code-review` (medium) on `e583fb5`: two findings, both verified and
fixed, each pinned by a test that fails on `e583fb5`:**

1. Nothing probes between cloud missions, so an old answer showed as a live
   green "reachable" (or "unreachable since" a time from yesterday, date
   dropped). The snapshot now carries `age_s` on the brain's own clock; the

2. 3.53's late check went through the 8 s confirmation client, so a cloud
   slower than 8 s but under 20 s failed late attempts it used to pass.

**`/code-review` (medium) on `402b025`:** one low finding, verified and

**Known limits:** a hang that shows as an async trigger call still out uses
the step's deadline on that wait, and the confirmation then fails as the
cloud's with no call of its own. httpx's deadline is per phase (slow connect
plus slow read can outlast the guard; the backstop then holds one call in
flight). The watch's "reachable" is the service's `/health`; Bedrock behind
it can still fail. ~~The Guide tab does not show `status.cloud`.~~ It does
since the follow-up below.

## Follow-up, 2026-10-09 (branch `plan/3.56-cloud-health-fixes`)

**An independent Thermos pass on merged 3.56 (`0c50af7^..e321705`) found
three issues; nothing moves the robot. All fixed, each pinned by a test that
fails on `dev` before the fix:**

1. **The watching page's Cloud row froze** (medium-low). After a mission
   ended the panel poll stopped, and the late-check watcher drew nothing
   while `late_confirmation` was `waiting`: the row never aged to "last
   seen" (scenario A) and never turned `reachable` when `/health` came back
   during a parked `arrived_unconfirmed` (scenario B). Every watcher poll
   now redraws it (`renderCloudRow`). `test_the_cloud_row_keeps_moving_while_a_parked_arrival_waits`.
2. **A Stop's answer had no `cloud`** (low), so the row went blank and stayed
   so. `POST /mission/stop` carries it, with a runner or idle; the twin
   leaves the row alone for a status without the field.
   `test_followup_a_stop_answers_with_the_cloud_row`.
3. **Two collectors could apply one landed call** (low). A `wait_inflight()`
   thread abandoned at its deadline and the tick thread could both pass
   `_collect_inflight()`'s unlocked check: an answer applied twice, or one
   failure counted twice by B3.2. The take is now atomic under
   `_collect_lock` (and `reset_epoch()` clears under it).
   `test_followup_two_collectors_take_a_landed_call_once`.

**The Guide tab shows the cloud too** (asked by the user): a `CLD` row in
