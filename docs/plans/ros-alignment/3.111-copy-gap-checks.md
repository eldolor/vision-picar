# 3.111 Why copying the oracle better does not search better: three desktop checks

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.111; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** Every learned goal chooser since 3.96's F-large lands at 0.55-0.56
mean reward on the 588 test houses in mode R (3.103.C: F-large 0.553,
F-rich 0.563, F-pool-W32 0.555), the oracle at 0.783. Copying the oracle
better has not moved that: 3.101's F-rich copies 43% of its choices to
F-large's 35% for +0.010, and 3.110 found 34 facts copy about as well as
42. Before 3.110's stage 2 (spot, three more choosers), the user,
2026-10-27: "Three checks run on the desktop first."

**What is known going in** (read before this was written, so recorded
here, not tested):

* The gap is travel, not finding (3.103.C's split, mode R): of F-pool-W32's
  0.228, finds 0.009, goals 0.062, metres 0.158; median 13.8 m driven on a
  found mission to the oracle's 8.4.
* PPO lifts every clone a long way on the 100 selection houses (each
  arm's `best.json` log, iteration -1 is the clone itself): mode R from
  0.20-0.40 to 0.54-0.64, e.g. F-rich 0.286 -> 0.644, F-pool-W32 0.270 ->
  0.594. And the clone's copying does not order the clones' rewards
  (F-rich copies 43.0% at 0.286, F-lean-S64 37.8% at 0.397).
* Three of the five PPO runs since 3.101 (F-rich, F-pool-W32, F-pool-W64)
  kept their **last** selection check, iteration 59 of 60.

**Houses.** The checks never touch the 588 test houses, so nothing here
spends them. They run on **200 diagnostic houses**, ProcTHOR `val` 0-99 and
200-299 (by `usable("val")` index), which no chooser since 3.96 trained,
selected or was tested on (3.96's training uses `val` 300-569, its
selection `val` 100-199), each in modes R and U.

**The choosers.** The four that reached the test houses, each at its
`best.json`: F-large (3.96), F-rich (3.101), F-lean-S64 (3.103.B),
F-pool-W32 (3.103.C), each run greedy through its own harness patch, as its
test run did.

## Check 1: which choices matter

Each chooser and the oracle drive the 200 diagnostic houses x R and U.
Every choice with two or more candidates logs the oracle's cost for every
candidate (its path in 0.30 m cells to the nearest target, 3.88's
`cost_at`), the chooser's pick and the oracle's pick. Per choice,
**regret** = (cost of the pick - the lowest cost) x 0.30 m; a pick from
which the target cannot be reached, when some candidate can, is counted
apart (a dead pick), not given a number.

* **Exact** agreement (the pick is the oracle's) and **near** agreement
  (regret <= 1.0 m).
* **Concentration:** the share of all regret carried by the costliest 10%
  of choices.
* **Link to the score:** per mission, the Spearman correlation between its
  summed regret and its reward shortfall to the oracle's mission in the
  same house and mode (both outcomes in).

**Criteria (written before any check runs; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **A few costly choices:** the costliest 10% of choices carry **>= 50%**
   of the regret, for every chooser.
2. **Regret tracks the score:** the per-mission Spearman is **>= 0.5** for
   every chooser; below **0.3** for every chooser reads as "the oracle's
   per-choice cost is not what the score pays for".

**Criteria:**

## Check 3: can the oracle be copied at all?

The oracle picks the candidate nearest the target by true path, which the
robot cannot know. How much of its choice hangs on that?

* **3a, fit against held out:** 3.110's clones at 17 facts (rung 0) and 42
  facts (rung 25), three seeds each: agreement on their own 37,064
  training choices against the 609 held out.
* **3b, near ties:** of the 609 held-out choices, the share where another
  candidate is within 1.0 m of the oracle's cost; the 42-fact clones'
  near agreement on them.
* **3c, one fact the robot can never have:** the oracle sets rebuilt (the
  same houses and harness as 3.101's, its 42 columns) with a 43rd column,
  `target_room`: 1 when the candidate's goal lies in a room that holds the
  target (the simulator's room cells and objects, ground truth). Checked
  before it is used: 37,064 / 609 choices, and the first 42 columns equal
  to the sets 3.110 used, element for element. Clones in F-large's shape
  (S32x32, 3.96's clone), seeds 3961-3963, on 42 columns and on 43, from
  the same sets. Diagnostic only: nothing with `target_room` ever reaches
  `brain/`.

**Criteria:**

5. **Not memorising:** 3a's training agreement within **5 points** of the
   held-out agreement at both rungs (more reads as fitting what does not
   generalise; reported either way).
6. **Where the target is decides the oracle:** 3c's 43-column mean
   held-out agreement **>= 10 points** above the 42-column mean, every
   seed of 43 above every seed of 42. Under **3 points** reads as "the
   target's room is not what the facts miss".

**What follows (each a proposal for the user, none started here):**

* 1 and 2 met: copy with each choice weighted by its regret, not one vote
  per choice.
* 2 under 0.3: the oracle's per-choice cost is the wrong teacher; reward
  only (PPO, or CEM as in 3.100 and 3.102) is the lever.
* 4 met: a longer PPO budget for the chooser already chosen, before any
  new facts.
* 6 met: most of the oracle's lead is knowing where the target is; no fact
  set closes it, and the useful ceiling is a chooser that knows only what
  the robot could (3.110's stage 2 is not proposed).
* 6 under 3 points with 5 met: the facts miss something else, and 3.110's
  stage 2 stays on the table.

**Out of scope:** the 588 test houses; anything in `brain/`; the board.
Cost: the desktop only, no spot.

  **What follows, as written above:** 1 failed, so not regret-weighted
  copying as the lead lever; 4 failed, so not a longer budget; 6 in the
  middle, so neither "no fact set closes it" nor a clean case for
  3.110's stage 2. What the numbers point to instead (proposals, none
  started): (a) the 78% near ties, a label that scores ties as ties (a
  soft target over every candidate within 1 m) rather than one
  arbitrary oracle pick; (b) the drop from 42-45% on the oracle's
  states to 16-20% on the choosers' own, DAgger-style labels on the

  facts and fell, 29% to 24%); (c) for PPO, more stable steps rather
  than more of them (iteration 99's 0.442 after 89's 0.597).
