# 3.54 Rooms from CLIP, few-shot on the user's own rooms

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.54; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.51 asked whether the CLIP RN50 the tiered policy already loads can
name the room from text prompts alone ("a photo of a living room"), and it
could not: 67.0% per frame, macro 39.7%, the living room and little else.
The cheaper next try is **few-shot**: instead of describing rooms in words,
show CLIP a handful of labelled frames from each of the user's OWN rooms and
assign a frame to the room whose examples it looks most like. The user chose
(2026-10-09) to check the 17 other labelled walks on disk for room coverage
before asking for a new recording.

## Protocol (fixed before any embedding is computed)

* **Classes:** living room, basement, home gym. Stair-hall and office frames
  are left out of the test (no walk to hold them out against).
* **Held out by walk:** leave-one-walk-out over the 15 walks containing any
  of the three rooms (3.51's four, these eleven). The walk under test never
  supplies a reference.
* **Shots:** K = 10 frames per room (primary). K = 3 and K = 30 are reported,
  not judged. References per room: the decided frames of that room in every
  other walk, pooled in (walk, frame) order, K picked at evenly spaced
  positions (`round(i * (n - 1) / (K - 1))`). Deterministic; no seed.
* **Classifier:** nearest centroid. Each room's reference is the mean of its
  K L2-normalised CLIP RN50 whole-frame image embeddings, renormalised; a
  frame goes to the room with the highest cosine.
* **Labels:** `room-labels-3.51.json` for 3.51's walks, `room-labels-3.54.json`
  for these.
* **Cloud mapping for criterion 3:** 3.51's synonyms, plus `home gym`, `gym`,
  `exercise room`, `workout room` -> home gym, and `basement`, `basement or
  recreation room`, `recreation room`, `rec room` -> basement. Only frames
  with a recorded `room_guess` count.
* **Reported, not judged:** zero-shot CLIP (3.51's prompt ensemble) over the
  same three classes, as the baseline few-shot must beat to be worth it.

**Criteria (written before measuring; CLAUDE.md section 7). 3.51's bars,
kept:**

1. **Frame accuracy** over every decided test frame of the three rooms,
   pooled across folds: **>= 80%**.
2. **Macro accuracy** (mean per-room recall over the three rooms):
   **>= 70%**.
3. **Not worse than the cloud:** on frames with a mapped cloud answer,
   few-shot accuracy >= the cloud's accuracy **minus 5 points**. Fewer than
   30 such frames: recorded as not measurable.
4. **Cost, laptop CPU p90, whole-frame encode + classify: <= 62 ms.** The
   encoder is 3.51's (measured 165 ms); the classify step is timed
   separately.
5. **Stability, paired with criteria 1-2:** a 5-frame majority vote cuts
   room switches per 100 frames by **>= 50%** and costs **<= 2 points** of
   accuracy, pooled.

**Known confounds, stated before measuring:** each room was recorded on
particular evenings (the living room only on 2026-09-07; the basement and
gym on 09-08, 09-12 and 09-13), so lighting and session differences ride
along with the room. Leave-one-walk-out removes the same walk, not the same
evening. All three rooms are in one house, and the basement and gym are
adjacent rooms, which makes this easier than a whole house with a kitchen
and bedrooms.
