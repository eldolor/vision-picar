# 3.70 Survey mode, missions on the robot's own saved map, and four end states shown on the phone (2026-10-11): criteria, written before building -- not yet confirmed by the user

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.70; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user made these decisions on 2026-10-11 while reviewing the
Claude Doc "ROS 2 for vision-picar". 3.50 owns how the map is kept: its
2026-10-11 block covers localisation, self-built maps and private storage.
This section owns the brain jobs that use the kept map: a survey, a mission
on a saved map, and how a mission ends. Nothing here is built. Every
number below is a proposal for the user to confirm or amend **before** the
first run (CLAUDE.md section 7). A value marked TBD is undecided by the user,
and its criterion cannot be run until it is set.

## Decided by the user (2026-10-11)

1. **The roles stay as they are.**
   * The brain chooses the next goal and decides when to stop.
   * nav2 only plans and drives each leg, through `NavigateToPose` (the
     bridge's `POST /goal`, R6).
   * `slam_toolbox` only maps and localises.
   * Frontier exploration stays in the brain (3.31's `explore` policy), not
     ROS's `explore_lite`. That restates open question 6 and 3.26; it is not
     a change.
2. **Survey mode, a new brain job.** It is the brain's own frontier loop with
   no target.
   * It is started from the phone, for a new house or after big changes.
   * It ends when no reachable frontier is left.
   * On the way, the camera and the LLM label rooms and note the objects
     the robot recognises. Both are saved with the map.
3. **Missions.** "Find X" loads the saved map, looks first where X was last
   seen or is likeliest to be, and then explores frontiers. A survey is
   optional: a mission in a house nobody surveyed still works, only more
   slowly.
4. **Four end states.** The brain decides them and reports them to the
   phone:
   * **Found:** the target was seen and confirmed, and the robot parks
     beside it.
   * **Exhausted:** no reachable frontier and no unvisited room is left.
   * **Out of budget:** a per-mission time limit or a battery floor was
     reached. The robot stops and reports where it looked.
   * **Cancelled:** a person stopped the mission from the phone.

   The limit values are **TBD**.
5. **Maps are self-built only** (the details are in 3.50). The robot is never
   given the simulator's floor plan. A first visit starts from an empty
   map, and later runs reload the robot's own saved map.

## What exists today (read at `origin/dev` `3db4e7d`)

* **Outcomes** (`control/mission_runner.py`): `found`, `searched` (3.31),
  `max_steps`, `stopped`, `failed`, `preempted`, `blocked`,
  `arrived_unconfirmed` (3.47), `room_reached`.
* **The budget is counted in steps,** not time: `max_steps` 120
  (`config/robot.yaml`), plus the cap on paid calls. No mission has a
  wall-clock limit. The brain reads no battery: the fake board's `v` is a
  constant 1200 (12.00 V, 3.25), and the real board reports `v` in every
  `T:1001` frame.
* **`explore` needs a target.** No mission runs without one.
* **Rooms on real frames** come only from the cloud's `room_guess`. That was
  77% right on 3.51's frames; local CLIP was 67% zero-shot (3.51), and
  few-shot (3.54) is not wired in. In the simulator, frames carry the
  sim's own `room` (3.2).
* **Objects on real frames:** none. 3.46's phase C closed on 2026-10-10,
  so the inventory records nothing from a camera frame. Only the
  simulator's detections fill it. The cloud's `important_objects` are
  names without a position (3.46 C).
* **Maps:** every restart is a new map. Nothing is saved yet (3.50).
* **No `explore` phase has ever ended on its own** in `home_first_floor`.
  All of 3.45's and 3.67's runs hit the 900 s cap, at a coverage of
  0.72-0.95.

## Conflicts with what the plan says now (for the user; not resolved here)

1. **Lifelong mode, and "localisation mode that adds rooms".** See 3.50's
   2026-10-11 block. 3.50 chose D on 2026-10-09 and ruled out lifelong mode,
   and question 6 says "Not lifelong mode". Upstream, localisation mode
   cannot add a room. **Resolved by the user 2026-10-11:** D stays, with a
   re-map triggered when the robot enters space the saved map calls
   unknown; lifelong mode stays out (3.50, item 2).
2. **Exhausted vs `searched`.** 3.31's `searched` means no reachable
   frontier is left, after the retry rule. Exhausted adds "no unvisited
   room". Should `searched` be renamed, or be reported as Exhausted? And
   with no room labels (no survey), is the frontier condition enough?
   **Decided by the user 2026-10-11: rename `searched` to `exhausted`**, so
   the code and the phone use one word (E1). With no room labels, the
   frontier condition alone ends it (E3); this is the plan's default and is
   still the user's to change.
3. **Out of budget vs `max_steps`.** Today's budget is a step count. The
   decision names a time limit and a battery floor. Does `max_steps` stay
   as a backstop under them, or go?
   **The user, 2026-10-11: 120 is too quick at a photo every 500 ms.**
   Checked: a step is one decision on one frame (`len(agent.history)` in
   `control/mission_runner.py`), so 120 steps is about **60 s** of
   searching at that rate -- far short of S1's proposed 30 min survey.
   The live explore demos already pass 200 and 400 (`tests/demo_explore.py`).
   **Decided by the user 2026-10-11:** the time limit and the battery floor are
   the budget. `max_steps` stays only as a runaway backstop derived from
   the time limit, not a fixed number: `mission_time_limit_s` / 0.5 s x 2
   (a 30 min limit gives 7,200 steps), so it can fire only if the clock
   check itself fails. Hitting it ends Out of budget, like the others.
4. **Cancelled vs `stopped` and `preempted`.** Stop from the phone ends a
   mission `stopped` today. A D-pad tap from the same phone ends it
   `preempted` (M4). Which of these is Cancelled?
   **Decided by the user 2026-10-11: both.** Either way a person ended the
   mission. The record keeps who did it in a `cancelled_by` field
   (`stop` or `dpad`), so the logs lose nothing (E5).
5. **Four end states vs the five others.** `failed` (B3.2, ROS down,
   `no_feedback`), `blocked` (3.31's stuck rule), `arrived_unconfirmed`
   (3.47) and `room_reached` are not among the four. This
   section assumes they stay as fault outcomes beside the four, and that
   `arrived_unconfirmed` is never shown as Found. Both assumptions are the
   user's to confirm.
   **Decided by the user 2026-10-11: both hold.** The four stay fault (or,
   for `room_reached`, goal) outcomes beside the four end states, each
   shown in its own style (E6). `arrived_unconfirmed` is never Found.
6. **"The camera/LLM labels rooms and notes objects" on the car means paid
   cloud calls.** No local namer passed (3.46 C), and local room labels
   were not adopted (3.51, 3.54). A survey's labels on real frames are
   therefore Bedrock calls. Their count and cost are estimated and approved
   before any live survey, under the user's standing rule.
   **Decided by the user 2026-10-11:** one cloud call each time the robot
   enters map space no label covers yet (a new room, as far as the car can
   tell), with a cap on calls per survey. The first live survey waits for
   the user's approval of a cost estimate. Sim surveys make no paid calls
   (S2).
7. **"Likeliest to be" has no source yet.** Nothing on `dev` holds a prior
   for where an object is likely to be. The learned version is 3.65's phase
   3, on branch `plan/3.65-rl-in-the-brain` (not on `dev`), which waits on
   3.50 and 3.46. M4 below is left open.
8. **"The map starts empty every session" does not appear in the plan in
   those words.** The nearest is 3.50's option A, "A fresh map every
   session (today)", which describes the code before 3.50 is built. It is
   annotated there, not rewritten: it is still true of the code.

## Part A -- Survey mode

**Design** (recorded so the criteria can be read against it): a mission
with no target and job `survey`. It runs the `explore` policy's frontier
loop (3.31, with 3.45's goal clearance) with the target gate off. Perception
feeds only the room labels and the inventory (3.46). The survey never ends
Found. Arbitration is unchanged: it is the brain, an autonomous driver
(3.23). The exact API shape is settled when it is built.

**S1. A survey runs with no target and ends Exhausted.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * Start from an empty map, 3 fresh starts each in `scaled_house`,
    `home_first_floor` and `complex_house`.
  * **9 of 9 end Exhausted.** An end needs 3.31's rule: every set-aside
    frontier has had its last retry, with K = 3.
  * Within **30 min** each (proposed; twice 3.45's 900 s cap).
  * Coverage at the end **>= 95%** of truth-reachable floor
    (`tests/demo_explore.coverage()`; 3.31 criterion 2's bar).
  * No stretch **>= 120 s** parked (3.45 criterion 1).
  * **0 contacts** on ground truth.
* *Sim tests:*
  * An in-process `FakeNav` sweep over ProcTHOR usable houses **200-299**
    (3.66's import). This set is unused: 3.66 and 3.69 hold 0-199. The bars
    are **>= 90 of 100** ending Exhausted and 100% ending with an outcome.
  * A unit test, red first: a survey never ends Found and never makes a
    target-confirmation call.
  * One live survey through the brain's HTTP API on the ROS stack in
    `home_first_floor`, which ends Exhausted.

**S2. Rooms are labelled during the survey and attached to the map.**
* *Acceptance (sim):*
  * The simulator's per-frame `room` stands in for the label source, so
    this measures where labels land, not whether rooms are recognised.
  * At least **95%** of each room's truth-reachable area carries that
    room's label on the saved map.
  * 0 cells carry a label from another room more than **0.3 m** inside a
    wall line.
* *Acceptance (car, deferred to the Rover):*
  * The labels come from the cloud's `room_guess` on keyframes. Their
    accuracy is recorded against a hand-labelled walk of the house, beside
    3.51's 77%. No bar is set until those frames exist.
  * **Calls a survey (decided 2026-10-11, conflict 6):** one `room_guess`
    call on entering space no label covers yet, never one per frame. The
    cap per survey is set from the sim survey's count of such entries
    (proposed: the 9-house median x 2), and reaching it leaves the rest
    unlabelled rather than ending the survey. The cost estimate (calls,
    tokens per call, price, total) is approved by the user before any live
    survey makes one.
  * *Sim test:* the count of label calls the sim survey would make is
    recorded per house, and a stub client counts that no call is made
    past the cap.
* *Sim test:* the survey sweep above, scoring labels against
  `build_world(...).rooms`.

**S3. Objects are noted during the survey.**
* *Acceptance (sim, `complex_house`, 20 surveys from spread starts):*
  3.46's bars, unchanged:
  * recall **>= 90%**, small objects **>= 80%**;
  * placement median **<= 0.20 m**, p90 **<= 0.50 m**;
  * duplicates **<= 1.2** per instance;
  * reported precision **>= 95%**.

  Judged on a fresh seed.
* *On the car:* only what 3.46 records from real frames (nothing today;
  conflict 6).
* *Sim test:* `tests/inventory_sweep.py` with the survey job.

**S4. Rooms and objects are saved with the map, under its `map_id`.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * A survey writes the map (3.50 criterion 10), the room labels and the
    inventory as one version under one `map_id`.
  * A container restart and a reload return all three equal to what was
    written:
    * labels cell for cell;
    * landmarks by id, position, belief and votes.
  * After a fall-back to a fresh map (3.50 criterion 8), **0** of the old
    labels or landmarks are used.
* *Sim test:* an offline round-trip test against a fake bridge (red first),
  and the live round trip inside 3.50 criterion 10's runs.

**S5. A survey is started from the phone.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * The twin has a control that starts a survey. It names the mission as a
    survey while it runs.
  * A D-pad tap still preempts it (M4, unchanged).
* *Tests:*
  * A UI test at 390 px, red first, run in its module's real order.
  * A phone-size screenshot (CLAUDE.md section 7).

## Part B -- Missions on a saved map

**M1. A mission loads the saved map, or starts empty on a first visit.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * With a saved map, the mission runs on its `map_id` and reloads it as
    3.50 specifies. With none, it starts from an empty map under a new
    `map_id`.
  * In both cases the map's first known cells come only from the robot's
    own scans (3.50 criterion 12).
* *Sim test:* a contract test over both cases against a fake bridge, red
  first.

**M2. "Find X" looks first where X was last seen.**
* *Acceptance (sim, `complex_house`):*
  * A survey, then 20 missions for small objects the survey reported, from
    spread starts.
  * Found **>= 95%**.
  * Where the inventory holds X at belief >= 0.8, the mission's **first**
    nav2 goal is the approach to that landmark in **100%** of missions.
  * Median time to Found **<= 50%** of the same missions on an unsurveyed
    house (proposed). Steps and distance are reported beside it.
* *Sim test:* `tests/inventory_sweep.py`'s missions, run twice (surveyed
  and not), with the same seeds.

**M3. A memory that is out of date costs a detour, never a wrong Found.**
* *Acceptance (sim):*
  * X is moved (`move_object`) after the survey.
  * Found **>= 95%**, at no worse than the unsurveyed rate.
  * The stale landmark's belief falls (3.46's miss rule).
  * **0** missions end Found on memory alone. Found still needs the live
    arrival rule and the cloud's yes (3.11, 3.47), unchanged.
* *Sim test:* the M2 sweep with every target moved between the survey and
  the mission, red first on a policy that trusts the memory.

**M4. "Likeliest to be": OPEN.** No source for a prior exists (conflict 7).
Its criteria are written when the user picks one: a fixed table of
room-by-object pairs, the cloud, or 3.65 phase 3. Until then a mission with
no last-seen landmark goes straight to frontiers. M5 measures that path.

**M5. A house nobody surveyed still works, more slowly.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * With no saved map and no survey, "find X" meets 3.31 criterion 1 in
    `home_first_floor`: Found in every room nav2 can plan into.
  * It reaches **>= 90 of 100** on ProcTHOR usable houses 200-299 (3.66's
    bar), in a `FakeNav` sweep.
  * Median time is recorded beside M2's surveyed number. Slower is
    expected, so time has no bar here.
* *Sim test:* 3.31's room sweep and the ProcTHOR sweep, unchanged but for
  the house set.

## Part C -- Four end states

**E1. The four are named, and every ending maps to one of them or to a
fault.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * `GET /mission/status` carries the end state (Found, Exhausted, Out of
    budget, Cancelled) and its reason.
  * `searched` is renamed `exhausted` everywhere it is written:
    `control/mission_runner.py`, `brain/explore.py`'s events, `brain_view`,
    the twin, the mission specs and AGENT-HARNESS. Records already saved
    with `searched` (mission logs, metrics rows, run JSON under
    `evaluations/`) are read as `exhausted`, never rewritten. A test pins
    that no `"searched"` outcome is written after the rename.
  * Each of today's other outcomes is assigned to one of the four or kept
    as a fault, as the user decides (conflicts 3-5). The table goes in the
    mission ARCHITECTURE spec, in the same commit as the code.
* *Sim test:* one `MissionRunner` test per end state, red first.
  `brain_view`'s `_OUTCOME_LEVEL` names each, which was 3.47's lesson for
  `searched`.

**E2. Found: seen, confirmed, parked beside it.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * Found only with 3.11's arrival rule (centred, lidar <= 0.40 m, two
    frames) **and** the cloud's yes (handoff 1a).
  * The wheels are at zero at the end.
  * 3.11's and 3.47's pinned sweeps pass unchanged: 0 Found farther than
    0.60 m, 0 Found with a dead cloud.
* *Sim test:* the existing arrival tests, plus S1's "a survey never ends
  Found".

**E3. Exhausted: nothing reachable left to look at.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * An absent target ends Exhausted, never `blocked` (3.31 criterion 2).
  * At least **95%** of truth-reachable floor is seen.
  * Where the map has room labels, every labelled room has been entered
    (the robot's centre inside it) before Exhausted.
* *Sim test:* 3.31's `absent` sweep, plus a test with one labelled room left
  unvisited and no frontiers, which must not end Exhausted (red first).

**E4. Out of budget: the robot stops and reports where it looked.**
* *Limits:* `mission_time_limit_s` **TBD**; `battery_floor_v` **TBD**.
* *Backstop (decided 2026-10-11, conflict 3):* `max_steps` is no longer a
  fixed 120. It is derived: `mission_time_limit_s` / 0.5 s x 2, and
  reaching it ends Out of budget (not `max_steps`). A sim test pins that a
  mission with the clock check disabled still stops at the derived count,
  and that no record is written with the outcome `max_steps`; old records
  are read as Out of budget, never rewritten.
  Battery is read from the board's `v`. The fake board must first drain `v`
  with a model; today it is a constant.
* *Acceptance (sim):*
  * With the time limit set to a test value of 120 s, the mission ends Out
    of budget within the limit plus one tick, and the wheels are at zero
    within **0.5 s**.
  * With the fake board's `v` falling below a test floor, the same end
    comes within **2 s**.
  * The report lists:
    * the rooms entered, equal to the truth list of rooms whose floor the
      robot's centre entered;
    * coverage, within **2 points** of `demo_explore.coverage()`;
    * the goal points reached;
    * the `map_id` version saved at the stop.
* *Sim test:* both triggers in process, red first. One live run through the
  brain's HTTP API on the ROS stack, with the time limit set low.

**E5. Cancelled: a person stopped it from the phone.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * Stop from the twin ends the mission Cancelled, with the wheels at zero
    within **0.5 s**.
  * A D-pad tap that takes over from the brain (M4) also ends it Cancelled
    (decided 2026-10-11, conflict 4). The record's `cancelled_by` is `stop`
    or `dpad`. `stopped` and `preempted` are no longer written; old records
    are read as Cancelled with their `cancelled_by` inferred, never
    rewritten.
  * It carries the same "where it looked" report as E4.
  * 3.61's Stop/Start race tests and 3.53's Stop tests pass unchanged.
* *Sim test:* the existing Stop tests, plus a report test, red first.

**E6. The phone shows each end state.**
* *Acceptance:*
  * The Remote brain panel (Sim tab) and the Guide tab's HUD show all four
    with distinct text and style:
    * Found: the ok style;
    * Exhausted and Out of budget: 3.53's warn style;
    * Cancelled: neutral.
  * The outcomes kept beside the four (conflict 5, decided 2026-10-11)
    keep today's styles (`web-twin/app.js`, `brain_view.py`
    `_OUTCOME_LEVEL`): `room_reached` ok, `blocked` and
    `arrived_unconfirmed` warn, `failed` error. `arrived_unconfirmed`
    never uses Found's text or style. `_OUTCOME_LEVEL` gains the new names
    (`exhausted`, `out_of_budget`, `cancelled`), so none falls to its
    unknown-outcome default.
  * Out of budget and Cancelled show the report's rooms and coverage.
  * No end state shows as an error, or as a lost link.
* *Tests:*
  * One UI test per state at 390 px, red first, run in module order.
  * A phone-size screenshot of each.
  * Deployed only once `service/static/sync.sh` reports `Completed`.
