# 3.119 PPO with per-choice credit: a learned critic, then a critic that sees the truth

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.119; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** Every explore-goal chooser since 3.96 lands at 0.55-0.59 mode-R
reward whatever its facts (3.101, 3.110, 3.117), shape or pool attention
(3.103), labels (3.114) or starting state (3.115), about 0.11 under the
oracle on 3.111's diagnostic houses. None of those changed how PPO assigns
credit, and 3.96's trainer has no critic: `stage_ppo` scores each mission
against the other rollouts of its house and mode (group-relative), and
`ppo_update` gives **every choice in a mission the same advantage**
(`evaluations/goal-learn-396/train.py`, `m["adv"]`). With a median of 4-9
goals a mission, a good choice in a bad mission is pushed down as hard as
the choice that sank it. The user's go, 2026-10-29, after a discussion of
AWS DeepRacer's lessons (an oracle's knowledge used inside training, not
only as labels): test per-choice credit, first with a learned critic, then
with a critic that also sees ground truth. Nothing the chooser sees on the
car changes; the critic exists only in training.

**Headroom** (3.117's, unchanged): on the 200 diagnostic houses the oracle
scores R 0.717 against W32's 0.604 (+0.113 [0.013, 0.203]) and U 0.773
against 0.355.

## Design

**Per-choice reward.** A mission's reward stays 3.65's:
`found - GOAL_COST x goals - METRE_COST x metres` (0.02, 0.01; a crash
`-CRASH_COST`). It is split over the recorded choices: choice t's reward is
the goals sent (including one-candidate goals, which are not recorded
choices) and metres driven from choice t up to choice t+1, at those costs;
the last choice also takes `found` (or the crash). Costs before the first
recorded choice belong to no choice and are left out of the per-choice
rewards. Recorded with each choice: goals sent and metres so far
(`nav.metres + robot._path_m`) at the moment it is made.

**The critic.** A state value V(s), separate from the chooser: an MLP
64 x 64 (tanh), its own Adam (lr 1e-3), MSE to the lambda-returns, trained
in the same `EPOCHS` (4) passes as the chooser. Its inputs:

* **A1 (the chooser's own facts):** the candidate pool's facts mean- and
  max-pooled (2 x 18), the pool size, goals sent and metres so far.
* **A2 (also the truth):** A1's inputs plus the mission's mode (R/U), the
  true floor-path distance from the robot to the target (m), the oracle's
  best candidate cost (m) and whether the target's room has been entered.
  The chooser's inputs are A1's and W32's, unchanged.

Advantages by GAE (gamma 1, the reward is an undiscounted episode sum;
lambda 0.95; V = 0 after the last choice), normalised over the iteration's
batch. No critic warm-up: iteration 0 uses an untrained critic (known
limit).

**Everything else is W32's PPO** (3.103.C): the start is W32's clone
(`evaluations/goal-learn-3103/cloud/ckpt/F-pool-W32/clone.pt`), 3.96's
budget unchanged (60 iterations, 48 houses x 8 rollouts, 4 epochs, lr
1e-3, clip 0.2, entropy 0.01, selection every 10 on the 100 selection
houses, best by mode-R selection reward), seed 3961. The best then drives
3.111's 200 diagnostic houses (`val` 0-99 and 200-299), R and U, every
choice logged, as 3.117 did. W32 itself is not re-run: 3.103 re-ran W64 on
another host to the digit, and its `best.json` and 3.111's rows are the
comparison.

**Checks before any arm runs** (nothing runs if one fails):

1. **Plumbing:** the new trainer in group-relative mode (no critic)
   reproduces W32's logged iteration 0 (`loss`, `entropy`, `clipped`,
   `mean_reward`) to the digit.
2. **The split:** on 8 sampled missions, the per-choice rewards plus the
   cost before the first choice sum to the mission reward within 1e-6.
3. **The truth inputs:** A2's true distance at the robot's start equals
   the harness's `pick_target` distance for those 8 missions.

**A3 (shaping), conditional.** Potential-based shaping, Phi(s) = -0.03 x
the true path distance robot -> target (m), added as Phi(s') - Phi(s) to
each choice's reward on top of A2. It runs **only if** A2 fails criterion 1
with its R point estimate at or above +0.02; otherwise it is not run. Rule
written here before A2 runs.

## Criteria (written before any arm runs; CLAUDE.md section 7)

Per arm (A1, A2, and A3 if it runs), paired by house against W32's
`best.json` (3.111's rows), 3.111's bootstrap (10,000 draws, seed 3119):

1. **Per-choice credit drives better:** diagnostic mode R, 95% interval
   above 0.
2. **Not at mode U's cost:** diagnostic mode U, interval lower end above
   -0.05.

**The test-house gate:** only an arm meeting **both** is proposed for the
588 test houses (one run, against F-large and W32 by 3.103's paired test),
and that run is still the user's call. **No arm meets both: the learned
chooser line is closed until the Rover's data exists** (the next lever then
is the target's room from real perception, not the learner).

Reported, not judged: the critic's explained variance per iteration, the
selection curve, the spread of advantages within a mission (zero under
group-relative), near agreement on own diagnostic choices (3.111's
measure), the oracle gap.

**Where it runs (the user's call before any arm):** the desktop, about
2.5-3 h of PPO per arm (3.117's k07: 8,718 s on 10 workers), 5-6 h for A1
and A2 in turn -- the desktop lost power under this load on 2026-10-28; or
AWS spot as 3.103.C (`c7i.16xlarge` ~$0.79/h, the two arms side by side in
~2 h, roughly $1-3 with checkpoints to S3).

**Out of scope:** the 588 test houses; anything in `brain/`; the board.
One seed per arm (known limit).
