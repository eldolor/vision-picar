# 3.45 Explore never sends goals into corners it cannot leave (2026-10-07): criteria, written before building -- confirmed by the user 2026-10-07

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.45; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.43 and 3.44 left furnished-home explore-then-tour runs parking
105-1119 s (bar < 120 s), with tours of 7, 7 and 0 of 9 goals. Neither the
stopping distance (3.44) nor nav2's inflation (3.43 part b) ended it, and
neither is re-tuned here. Re-read through 3.45's per-phase judge
(`evaluations/slam-345/judge.py`), the six recorded runs
(`evaluations/slam-343/inflate-home{1,2,3}.json`, `slam-344/creep-home{1,2,3}.json`)
agree on where the time goes:

* **Every run's longest stall begins in the explore phase**, never in the
  tour. The tour's own longest stall is 15-69 s, except in 3.44 run 3,
  where explore parked the robot at (2.53, 8.74) at 592 s and the tour
  inherited it (0 of 9 goals). That is why the tour scored 0 there.
* **The same places recur**: the foyer 1.3 m south of the start
  (4.8, 12.56), 21 s into four of the six runs, for 92-134 s; beside the
  living-room sofa (0.8, 8.06); and (2.35, 7.9) and (0.67, 11.97) in the
  living room and den. A goal-choice problem repeats where the map looks
  the same; that matches 3.43's finding that explore sends goals into corners
  the lidar cannot see into.
* **No explore phase ended on its own**: all six hit the 900 s cap, with
  coverage 0.32-0.72 of reachable floor.

What explore chose before each stall cannot be read off those runs: the
brain logged nothing at INFO, and each stack overwrote the last one's logs.

**Step 1, built first (commit `ebd5a82` and this one).** Live runs are now
observable:
* `PICAR_LOG_LEVEL` (`robot/identity.configure_logging()`, both servers;
  unset changes nothing). The stack runs the brain at INFO and the robot
  server at WARNING, with epoch time stamps.
* `ExploreAgent.events`: every goal sent (with its rank, score, path and
  frontier size), refused or ended (state, seconds, distance moved), and
  every escape and verb with its result, also logged as `explore {json}`.
* `tests/demo_explore.stack()` gives each stack its own
  `$EXPLORE_LOGDIR/run-<time>/` for the robot, brain and ROS logs and the
  run JSON.
* `demo_slam_home` adds phase and per-goal times, the true heading in the
  1 Hz series, and SLAM map + pose + truth snapshots every 20 s
  (`maps.jsonl.gz`).

**Scope.** Goal choice and goal-failure handling in `brain/explore.py` and
`brain/frontier.py`. `robot/safety.py`, `nav2.yaml` and `slam.yaml` do not
change. The ROS image is `vision-picar-ros:plan-3.45`, a tag of `:latest`
whose 13 source files under `service/slam/src` were checked identical to
this branch.

**Criteria** (ground truth throughout; CLAUDE.md section 7). Today's code
(dev plus step 1's logging, which changes no decision) is measured first on
criteria 1-5, then the fix, on the same instruments:

1. **No stalls, live.** Three fresh furnished-home runs,
   `python -m tests.demo_slam_home 3 --tour 1 --explore-first 900`, judged
   by `evaluations/slam-343/parked.py` unchanged. In every run, the longest
   stretch within 5 cm of one spot is **< 120 s** over the whole run,
   explore and tour alike.
2. **Tour.** Each run's tour reaches **>= 8 of 9** goals.
3. **SLAM holds (3.40's bars):** `evaluations/slam-340/judge.py` passes in
   all three runs: east max <= 0.20 m, final <= 0.10 m, 0 jumps, each judged
   room's walls >= 90% true.
4. **Progress: explore coverage must not drop.** Coverage is
   `tests/demo_explore.coverage()`, the share of truth-reachable floor
   known at the end of the 900 s explore phase.
   * The median of the three runs is **>= the median of today's three**.
   * No run is **below today's lowest**.
5. **Goal choice, in process, on the maps explore really had.**
   * `evaluations/slam-345/goal_sweep.py` replays explore's choice of the
     next frontier or view goal on every map snapshot of today's three
     runs. That set is pinned as recorded; it does not grow.
   * It checks each chosen goal point on ground truth with the real safety
     layer (`tests/escape_sweep.wedge_kind`, every 10 deg of heading).
   * A goal is **leavable** when, at every heading, forward or reverse is
     allowed.
   * Bar: **>= 95% of chosen goals leavable**.
   * Progress half: the share of snapshots that offer a goal at all is no
     more than 5 points below today's.
   * **If today's code already meets the 95% bar**, the stall is not at
     the goal point but on the way to it. I bring that, with the logs,
     back to you before building, rather than build against this proxy.
6. **Nothing earlier regresses.**
   * R6 on the scaled house 6/6
     (`evaluations/slam-340/with_stack.py scaled_house '' -- python -m tests.demo_nav_goals`).
   * `tests/test_explore.py` and the full suite pass (`plan_section.py ready`).
   * No diff in `robot/safety.py`, `nav2.yaml` or `slam.yaml`.
7. **Pinned and specified.**
   * A test of the goal-choice change, confirmed red on today's code.
   * `brain/explore.py`'s goal choice gets its ARCHITECTURE and ENGINEERING
     spec text (it has had none since 3.31), in the same commit as the code.
8. **The tour instrument is judged apart from explore.**
   * `evaluations/slam-345/judge.py` puts each stall in its phase and tour
     goal. A stall that starts in explore and runs into the tour is
     explore's.
   * Each tour failure is recorded with its state, time and end error.
   * Tour goal 6 (dining room) has failed in every run since 3.40. Goal 7
     (living room) newly aborts right after it. Both are recorded each run
     from the run's own nav2 log, as the tour's problem and not explore's.
   * Criterion 2's 8-of-9 bar is unchanged. While goal 6 always fails, it
     needs every other goal to succeed.

What the new logs show. **Every stall of 60 s or more (13 across the three
runs) is in the explore phase and follows a frontier goal.** The robot
server was refusing nav2's own moves for the whole stall: 1 189-1 959 forwards
at the 20 cm bar, or 400-2 900 turns. The robot is near the goal and the
safety layer will not let nav2 finish or leave, until `GOAL_TIMEOUT_S`
(120 s) cancels it. That timeout alone is the bar. The foyer goal
(4.16-4.73, 12.6) was chosen in all three runs and stalled 69-120 s each
time. Explore's goal ends: 33 succeeded, 11 timed out, 11 aborted.

*Criterion 5 on today's code -- FAILED: 32 / 132 = 24.2% leavable*
(`goal_sweep.py` over the three runs' 132 snapshots; every snapshot offered
a goal). The unleavable goals are of two kinds:
* **On furniture on truth** (all 36 headings overlap). The goal point is
  3-10 cm from a true surface, the map shows unknown one cell away, and the
  nearest occupied cell is 0.25 m off.
* **Too tight for the safety layer** at some headings: 0.21-0.28 m from
  true surfaces, so both straight moves are refused.

The goal clearance, 3.31's `GOAL_CLEARANCE_M` = 0.22 m, was sized for
nav2's 0.12 m inflation. To drive away from a surface at any heading, the
chassis needs its half-length (0.1265 m) plus the 20 cm bar: 0.3265 m.

*Criterion 8 on the baseline* (`evaluations/slam-345/tour_goals.py`):
* **Tour goal 6 (dining room) cannot succeed as written.** At its point,
  (9.75, 9.45) m, the chassis overlaps the dining furniture on truth by up
  to 2.1 cm. nav2 logs "failed to create plan ... to (1.80, -4.50)" in every
  run, and the robot ends 1.00-1.12 m short. This is the instrument's
  defect, not explore's or nav2's. It is recorded, not fixed, here.
* Goal 7 succeeded in all three runs.
* Run 2's goal 3 (garage hall) timed out on the tour's own path: nav2
  "failed to make progress" five times while the robot server refused
  1 899 forwards. It was not inherited from explore.
