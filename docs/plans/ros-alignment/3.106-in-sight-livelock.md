# 3.106 Explore in the approach band, the target in sight but never found: bounded, not a livelock (house 1395)

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.106; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.96's criterion 5 failed on one mission: house `train:1395`
(mode R, a box) under F-large never ended; the 5,000-tick cap stopped it.
The user picked it from the open list (2026-10-26). Traced on `dev`
(`371edb6`): explore stands 0.64 m from the box, dead ahead (0.2 deg),
inside its approach band (`_at_target`, 0.70 m), with a dining-table leg
0.44 m off. The simulator's detector reports the box (any visible part,
`sim/grid_world.py` `_detections`), but its found rule (`objects_visible`,
the renderer's one ray to the object's centre) does not: the table hides the
centre. So every tick explore sees the target, has no close-in (that is the
tiers' perception path only, 3.80.G), and falls to `_back_to_sighting`'s
"here and not seeing it" branch, which clears the sighting and queues a
FACE that answers "already facing it". Nothing counts a failure, the
retry rule never lets go, and a WAIT passes no simulated time, so the time
limit never ends it either. On the car the wall clock would end it
`out_of_budget` at 1,200 s; plain explore's frame sightings exist only in
the simulator (3.76 E's open item), so this is a sweep-and-sim bug, but it
stops a sim mission dead.

**The change.** In explore, count consecutive ticks that are in the band,
see the target this tick, have no goal live and nothing to close in with.
At `CLOSE_IN_REFUSED` (3) such ticks the sighting counts one retry failure
and the robot backs off, as close-in does at its own refusal limit. The
first of those ticks still turns to face it (a target at the frame's edge
is found once faced). Explore then moves on; the retry rule (3 failures,
30 s cooldown) bounds the returns.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. House `train:1395`, mode R, under F-large (3.96's harness,
   `evaluations/goal-learn-396/train.py` `mission`) and under the rule ends
   under the tick cap with an outcome (`found`, `exhausted` or
   `out_of_budget`), not `capped`. Red on `dev` first for F-large. A test
   in the fast tier pins the bound itself (in sight, in the band, never
   found: a failure and a back-off within 3 ticks; a target at the frame's
   edge is still faced first).
2. No found mission lost: the rule arm on 3.96's 588 test houses (`train`
   1000-1999, modes R and U, `c.py`'s houses) finds at least as many as
   recorded there (R 570, U 582), median goals no higher (R 7, U 9).
3. The explore sweeps' pinned numbers hold (`test_procthor_sweep`,
   `test_goal_policy_sweep`, `test_frontier_fast_sweep`,
   `test_survey_sweep`, `test_inventory_mission`); a pin that moves is
   explained house by house or this fails. The fast tier passes.
