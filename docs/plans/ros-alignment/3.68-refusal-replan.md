# 3.68 Replan around a spot the safety layer keeps refusing (2026-10-11): built; FAILED (goal 6 0/3), not adopted

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.68; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.67 showed that nav2's parameters cannot fix the garage exit
(tour goal 6, 0/3 under three candidates). nav2 plans down a 0.305 m slot
beside the car toward a wall the lidar never mapped. The robot server's
safety layer will not drive a 0.231 m chassis plus its side margins into
that slot, so it refuses every forward move (1,203-1,218 refusals a run)
and nav2 keeps the same plan for the rest of the 120 s. Nothing tells ROS
about a refusal: the ros2_control plugin ignores the reply to its wheel
post. The planner cannot learn what the safety layer knows.

**The idea.** The robot server, which owns the refusal, notices a wedge,
and nav2 is given a keepout spot where the robot was trying to go, so its
planner routes around it. Judgement stays outside ROS (CLAUDE.md rule 3):
the robot server decides what is a wedge and where the spot goes; the
bridge only publishes a mask that nav2's stock `KeepoutFilter` reads.

**Design.**

1. **Wedge watch (`robot/wedge_watch.py`, plain Python).** It is fed every
   wheel post from driver `ros`: was a forward command zeroed by
   `vet_wheel_velocity` ("forward clamped")? A run of forward clamps with
   no forward command executed unclamped, and no gap longer than
   `GAP_S` (1.0 s), that lasts `HOLD_S` (3.0 s) is a wedge. By definition
   the robot made no forward progress in that run; it may only have
   turned. It fires once, then re-arms for another `HOLD_S`.
2. **The spot.** On a wedge, while a nav2 goal is pending or active, the
   server reads the pose and places a disc of radius `SPOT_R_M` (0.15 m)
   centred `SPOT_AHEAD_M` (0.25 m) ahead of the robot's centre along its
   heading. The disc covers where the robot's centre was trying to go
   (0.10-0.40 m ahead) and leaves its own cell free, so nav2 can still
   plan from where it stands. The mark is sent off the 20 Hz post's
   thread, so the plugin's control loop never waits on it.
3. **Lifetime.** Each spot lives `SPOT_TTL_S` (300 s); at most
   `MAX_SPOTS` (8), the oldest dropped first. A wrong mark can block a
   real passage only for that long. A new SLAM session (a restarted
   container) starts with none.
4. **The wall.** One new bridge route, `POST /nav/keepout {spots: [{x_m,
   y_m, r_m, ttl_s}]}`, which replaces the whole set. The bridge holds the
   expiry timer (plumbing: it honours a lifetime it is given) and
   republishes when a spot expires. It publishes `nav2_msgs/CostmapFilterInfo`
   (type 0, keepout) on `/costmap_filter_info` and an `OccupancyGrid` mask
   on `/keepout_mask`, both latched: 100 inside a disc, 0 elsewhere, in
   the `map` frame. The route budget rises from 13 to 14 in the same
   commit, with the reason. `world/ros_world.py` `set_keepout` converts
   from the house frame, as `set_goal` does.
5. **nav2.** Both costmaps get `filters: [keepout_filter]`
   (`nav2_costmap_2d::KeepoutFilter`, in the image already). Filters apply
   after inflation, so a spot is lethal to the robot's centre, which is
   what a disc around the centre's path means.

What it cannot do: it does not move the robot. If the robot is already
deep inside a slot where it cannot turn, the replan can still fail; the
criteria measure that.

**Instrument.** 3.67's, unchanged: three fresh furnished-home runs
(`python -m tests.demo_slam_home 3 --tour 1 --explore-first 900 --nav
<yaml>`) on this branch's image `vision-picar-ros:plan-3.68`, judged by
`evaluations/slam-343/parked.py`, `evaluations/slam-345/tour_goals.py` and
`evaluations/slam-340/judge.py`; then R6 on the scaled house. The config is
3.67's candidate A (inflation 0.35, cost scaling 5) plus the keepout
filter: `evaluations/slam-368/nav2-inflate-keepout.yaml`. Every mark is
logged by the robot server (`keepout:` lines in `explore_robot.log`).

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **The garage exit.** Goal 6 `succeeded` in **3 of 3** runs, ending
   <= 0.15 m from its point. No run is replaced.
2. **Tour.** Each run reaches **>= 8 of 9** goals.
3. **No stalls.** In every run the longest stretch within 5 cm of one spot
   is **< 120 s** (`parked.py`).
4. **Explore's progress holds.** Coverage at 900 s: median **>= 0.83**,
   no run below **0.68** (3.45's bars, as in 3.67).
5. **SLAM holds:** `judge.py` passes in all three runs.
6. **R6 on the scaled house 6/6**, and **no keepout mark** placed during
   it (that house has no wedge; a mark there is a false alarm).
7. **Marks are few and local.** At most **5 marks per run**, each recorded
   with its position; every mark during the tour lies within 1.0 m of a
   place where forward moves were refused (by construction) and none
   lies in a doorway of the house (0.9 m doors, read on ground truth).
8. **Safety is untouched.** The safety layer's vetting is unchanged (no
   diff to `robot/safety.py`); the suite passes; unit tests pin the
   watch (fires after 3 s of forward clamps; not on a brief clamp; not
   when a forward command executes between; not with no goal in
   progress) and the spot's frame conversion.

**If 1-8 are all met:** candidate A's inflation and the keepout filter go
into `nav2.yaml` (both costmaps), the ROS engineering spec says so in the
same commit, and `:latest` is rebuilt from `dev` after the merge. **If any
fails:** recorded as failed. The keepout machinery stays off unless its
filter is configured, so a failed run changes nothing live.

### Amendment 1, from code review, before any measurement (2026-10-11)

Both reviewers (`/code-review` and the Thermos bug pass) found that a disc
centred 0.25 m ahead with a 0.15 m radius reaches 2.6 cm under the
chassis' own nose; with the filter also in the LOCAL costmap, the
controller's and recoveries' footprint checks would then see the robot
standing in lethal cells and refuse even a turn. Changed before measuring:
`SPOT_AHEAD_M` 0.30 m (the disc covers the centre's next 0.15-0.45 m and
clears the nose and corners, pinned by a test), and the `KeepoutFilter` in
the **global costmap only** (the planner routes around a spot; the local
footprint checks never see it). Also fixed: a stale mask latched by an
expiry racing a new set, two marks posted out of order, a spot sent with
`ttl_s` 0 (the bridge would refuse the set), a refused mark logged as
marked, and a wedge counted on a post that then failed. Known and kept: a
spot outlives its goal for its 300 s; criterion 7 watches for a mark in a
doorway. Criteria 1-8 unchanged.

  0.953). **5 -- met.** **6 -- met:** R6 6/6, 9-10 cm from each goal,

  opening, disc radius 0.15 m). **8 -- met** (no diff to
  `robot/safety.py`, suite and the 26 new tests pass).
* **The garage exit changed but did not succeed.** In run 1 and run 3
  the watch fired once in the garage's north-west corner, (10.4, 8.9) m,
  where 3.67 A wedged for 100 s. nav2 backed up, replanned and left: goal
  6 now moves 4.4-6.8 m (3.67 A: 4.2-4.3 m) with 130-194 forward refusals
  (3.67 A: 1,203-1,218). It then ran out of its 120 s on the way to the
  dining room. In run 2 the planner reported "failed to create plan" for
  goal 6's point in its last 10 s. The wedge was broken; the goal was
  still not reached in time.
* **Where the marks came from: explore, not the tour.** 145 of 149 marks
  were placed during the 900 s of explore, at a few spots: the robot sat
  still and the watch re-fired every 3 s for up to 90 s at the same place
  (run 1: 18 marks at (5.59, 2.97); run 3: 30 at (8.2, 4.4)). A mark there
  did not get the robot moving: the planner routed around the disc, but
  the controller (no filter in the local costmap, amendment 1) kept
  sending forward commands that the safety layer zeroed. Run 1's 123 s
  park is at one of these spots. Marks the planner cannot use make the
  watch's own count meaningless, and filling all 8 slots at one spot may
  have evicted a useful mark elsewhere.
* **What would have to change (not built; a new section with its own
  criteria):** mark only once per spot (skip a spot within a radius of a
  live one); an action the controller cannot override when a mark does
  not help (a backup, as nav2's own recovery did in the garage); and a
  longer goal-6 budget is NOT a fix (criterion 1 is the 120 s tour).
* **Decision by the rule written before measuring:** `nav2.yaml` is
  unchanged; candidate A and the keepout filter are not adopted. The code
  (wedge watch, `POST /nav/keepout`, the 14th route) stays on branch
  `plan/3.68-refusal-replan` and is not merged: it would spend a bridge
  route on a feature no config uses. These records go to `dev`.
