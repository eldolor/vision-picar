# 3.97 A person's move ends a nav2 goal, under every drive

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.97; index: [README.md](README.md).
Reserved as "a person's nudge ... the twin says the goal resumes"; the
user's decision changed before it was built (below).*

**Why.** `docs-review/SPEC-REVIEW-3.md` fix 11 left "should a person's verb
cancel a goal?" to the user. **The facts, as read on `dev` 2026-10-24:**
only a Stop or `DELETE /world/goal` ended a goal in the robot server; under
`drive: ros` the bridge also cancelled on any non-zero `twin-dpad` twist
(3.15, `bridge.py` `cmd_vel()`), and every person drives on that input
(`ros_input_for`, handoff 3a); under `drive: direct` with a ROS world a
person's verb won only while it ran, and nav2 drove on to the goal after.

**Decided by the user, 2026-10-24.** First "keep today: nudge, then resume"
with a twin label (built red-first, then dropped unmerged), on my wrong
summary that no mode cancelled. Put again with the facts above: **cancel in
both modes.** Costs of the rejected nudge-and-resume, as put: the robot sets
off by itself after a release; changing one's mind takes a Stop; nav2's
progress checker (0.10 m in 15 s) can abort a goal under a long, still
take-over, so "resume" is not sure. Cost of the choice: a nudge needs a re-tap.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. Under `drive: direct` with a ROS world, a person's (`twin-dpad`,
   `teleop-operator`, unnamed) FORWARD or turn during an active goal runs
   and ends the goal; nav2 does not drive on after the stop hold. Red on
   `dev` first.
2. A camera pan (LOOK_*) and the brain's verb leave the goal; a person's
   move latches nothing (the brain is not refused `person_stop`); a tap
   right after a move is accepted and drives.
3. The Stop, goal, ROS-drive and keepout suites unchanged.
4. Live, owed to a session with the ROS stack: under `drive: ros` a
   D-pad move during a tapped goal ends it (3.15's measured 0.04-0.05 s,
   re-read on today's image), and the same under `drive: direct` with
   `WORLD_MODE=ros`.

drive; a run that dies before both taps is re-run once and the death
recorded. `evaluations/person-move-397/`.

sent concurrently with that press is answered `stopped` (3.82's rule); the
start of the loop costs one bridge read on a person's move in a ROS world
(arbitration already makes one).
