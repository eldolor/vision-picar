# 3.89 Explore's escape creep outlives RemoteRobot's 10 s timeout under drive: ros

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.89; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.88 A's live run 2 (2026-10-20, `home_first_floor`, `drive: ros`)
ended `failed` at 759 s with 0 contacts. In the dining-room wedge the escape
(3.85: REVERSE refused at 1.0 cm, the front open) drove FORWARD at speed 50
to the 20 cm bar (19.5 cm, depth grid), then crept FORWARD
(`CREEP_SPEED = 5`, 3.44). The creep reached the 3 cm line 10.26 s after it
was sent (robot log: `forward clamped: 3.0cm <= 3cm` at +10.26 s), so the
brain's `POST /action` hit `RemoteRobot`'s 10 s read timeout
(`DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_S`) at +10.39 s and the step failed the mission. 16.5 cm in
10.3 s is 1.6 cm/s, not the 3 cm/s the creep is asked at; the same run's
REVERSE creep covered 17.1 cm in 8.0 s (2.1 cm/s). The comment beside
`CREEP_SPEED` budgets "at most 17 cm to the 3 cm line, ~6 s" -- true only at
the asked speed. Records: `evaluations/goal-tap-388/live-2026-10-20/run2.json`;
logs `/tmp/vision-picar-3.88/run-20261020-154748/`.

**Criteria (written before measuring; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Diagnosis (a record, no bar).** For a creep verb (speed 5) toward a wall
   from the 20 cm bar, the wall-clock time from `POST /action` to its reply
   and where it goes -- travel at the achieved speed vs the asked 0.03 m/s,
   the stall wait (`VERB_STALL_S`), the settle passes, the stop hold, the
   twist relay -- measured under `drive: ros` and under the sim's own drive
   on the live stack (`tests/demo_explore.stack()`, this section's ports and
   domain), at least 10 creeps each way per drive.
2. **Stability.** No escape verb's `POST /action` outlives the brain's
   request timeout: over at least 40 wedge creeps under `drive: ros`
   (FORWARD and REVERSE, from 19-20 cm to the 3 cm line), the longest reply
   is at most 8.0 s (2 s of margin under 10 s), and 0 time out. Counted from
   the robot server's own timing of each `/action`, not the brain's.
   *Amendment 1 (2026-10-21, before any criterion-2 run):* the robot server
   logs at WARNING in `stack()`, so it records no verb timing; each
   `/action` is timed by the caller on the same machine instead
   (`evaluations/escape-creep-389/measure.py`), which is an upper bound on
   the server's own time. Only creeps that start at the bar (>= 15 cm of
   room) count.
3. **Progress.** The escape still frees the robot: `tests/escape_sweep.py`
   (in process, direct drive) keeps its pinned freed shares; a creep still
   ends at the 3 cm line (within 1 cm, on the depth grid) when nothing is
   in the way, so it gains what it gained before. Live: 3 explore missions
   in `home_first_floor` under `drive: ros` all end `found`, and at least one
   mission's escape creep runs (from its `creep` events) without a timeout.
   If no live mission wedges, a scripted wedge in the dining room (the robot
   driven there by nav2 goals, then the escape queued) counts for the creep.
4. **Safety, on ground truth.** `tests/footprint_sweep.py` unchanged (its
   pinned numbers hold); 0 contacts and 0 inside samples in every live run
   (ground-truth chassis gap, as 3.88's runner records).
5. **The sweep.** The 100-house ProcTHOR sweep pin (99) still holds.
6. **No safety loosening.** No veto threshold, margin or source changes
   (`robot/safety.py`'s bars, `CREEP_MARGIN_CM`, `VERB_STALL_S`); the fix
   is in how the escape plans its verb or how long the brain waits, argued
   below if it is the second.
