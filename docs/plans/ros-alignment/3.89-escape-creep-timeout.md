## The fix, and why not a longer timeout

A verb on the wall clock ends at `VERB_MAX_S` (7.0 s, `robot/interface.py`),
in `carry_out_verb` and in `ros_drive._run`'s deadline; only when that cap is
the deadline that bound does the reply say `stopped_short: "timeout"`. The
escape (`brain/explore.py`) sends a creep cut that way again, the same way,
up to `CREEP_PIECES` (3) pieces, and only while more than `CREEP_ROOM_CM`
(1 cm) remains past the 3 cm line, so it still reaches the line.

Raising `RemoteRobot`'s 10 s would also have ended the failure, but it is
B3.2's own guard: the time a robot server may hang a brain's step before the
step fails. A longer one delays every real hang's notice, on every verb, to
cover one slow verb. A bounded verb keeps that notice at 10 s and makes the
bound the robot server's promise (7 s + a settle + the reply, <= 8 s,
`test_the_cap_sits_inside_the_request_timeout_with_room_for_the_reply`).
No safety bar moved (criterion 6): the slow zone, the 3 cm line, the 20 cm
bar and `VERB_STALL_S` are as on `dev`.

Known limits (in `docs/engineering/body/ENGINEERING.md`): a slow bridge can
add to the bound; turns longer than ~92 deg at their speed are capped (no
policy sends one); ROS replies still carry no `stalled` marker (as on `dev`).
