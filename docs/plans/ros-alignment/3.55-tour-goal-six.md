# 3.55 The tour's dining-room goal stands on reachable floor (2026-10-09): criteria, written before building

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.55; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The furnished-home tour (`tests/demo_nav_goals.HOME_GOALS`, run by
`demo_slam_home --tour 1`) has failed goal 6, "dining room" at
(31.0, 30.0) ft = (9.75, 9.45) m, in every run since 3.40, including all six
of 3.45's. 3.45's criterion 8 found why
(`evaluations/slam-345/tour_goals.py`):
* The chassis at that point overlaps the dining furniture on ground truth,
  by up to 2.1 cm.
* nav2 logs "failed to create plan ... to (1.80, -4.50)", and the robot
  ends 1.00-1.12 m short.

So the tour's ">= 8 of 9" bar means "every other goal must pass", and a
failed goal 6 says nothing about navigation. The fix is to the instrument
only: no robot, brain or nav2 code changes.

**Today, from the record (no new measurement needed).** Goal 6 failed in
every recorded run since 3.40: 3.43's three, 3.44's three, and 3.45's six
(`evaluations/slam-343`, `slam-344`, `slam-345`). The other eight points:
chassis-to-truth gap at their worst heading 7.8-68 cm, none overlapping.

**Criteria:**

1. **Every tour point can be stood on and left.** For all nine
   `HOME_GOALS`, on ground truth:
   * the chassis overlaps nothing at any heading (every 10 deg);
   * at every heading, forward or reverse is allowed by the real safety
     layer (`goal_sweep.leavable`).
   Pinned by a test, red on today's goal 6.
2. **Goal 6 still tests the same journey.**
   * The new point lies in the dining room's own cells (`build_world(...).rooms`).
   * It is within 1.0 m of the old point.
   * It is the eligible point nearest the old one, chosen by a stated
     rule, not by hand.
   * The other eight points do not change.
3. **Goal 6 succeeds live.** Three fresh tour-only runs
   (`python -m tests.demo_slam_home 3 --tour 1`, no explore; the tour maps
   as it goes, as in 3.39-3.40).
   * Goal 6 `succeeded` in **3 of 3**, ending <= 0.15 m from its point.
   * A goal 6 that fails because an EARLIER goal left nav2 wedged is
     recorded as such (`tour_goals.py`), and that run is replaced by a
     fresh one, at most twice. The garage wedge (3.45) is not this
     section's subject.
4. **Nothing else changes.** 3.40's SLAM bars hold in those runs
   (`slam-340/judge.py`), and the full suite passes. The tour runs
   only on this branch's image tag (`:latest` content).
5. **The record is re-based.** 3.45's criterion 2 and every earlier
   tour count (8 of 9 "at best") were taken with an unreachable goal 6.
   This section says so in its result, so later tour counts are not
   compared against them as if goal 6 could have passed.
