# 3.108 The local VLM on by default on the board

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.108; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user, 2026-10-26: "I want the local VLM on by default on the
board." Since 3.80.B, `picar-local-vlm` (Qwen2.5-VL-3B VE under TensorRT
Edge-LLM, `127.0.0.1:8005`) was installed and not enabled, and the brain
used it only with `tier_local_vlm_url` / `LOCAL_VLM_URL` set, which nothing
on the board set: the board's brain reported `tier_local_vlm: false`.

**The change.** On the board only (the laptop, the suite and the cloud have
no local VLM, so `config/robot.yaml`'s default stays empty):

* `tools/jetson/systemd/picar-stack.service` sets
  `LOCAL_VLM_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8005`. The stack does not wait for the
  VLM: one loading, down or killed leaves the stack up, and identity goes
  to the cloud tier, as 3.80 designed.
* `tools/jetson/install_services.sh` enables and restarts `picar-local-vlm`
  as its own step after the stack and the tunnel, so a VLM problem never
  fails their install; with no `~/.vision-picar-local-vlm.env` it warns and
  leaves the VLM alone.
* `tools/jetson/README.md` section 5 says so; the update steps no longer
  restart the VLM a second time (every start counts against its limit of 4
  in 15 min).
* The benches (`headroom_identity.sh`, `headroom_mission.sh`,
  `bench_local_vlm.sh`) runtime-mask the VLM for their run, since their
  `isolate` would now start an enabled one, and put it back as found.

**Known limit (the review, kept):** with `LOCAL_VLM_URL` always set, a VLM
that is down or past its start limit still reads as configured: the brain's
"explore needs a local tier" guard lets a `tier_search: explore` mission
start, and each local question then goes to the cloud (paid) instead.
Before 3.108 that mission was refused. Checking the VLM's `/health` at the
start would close it; not built here.

**Review amendment (before applying on the board):** criterion 2 as first
written could not fail (`tier_local_vlm` is only "the URL is set"); it now
asks through the brain's own client.

**Criteria (written before applying it on the board; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **Enabled and up:** `picar-local-vlm`, `picar-stack` and `picar-ngrok`
   `enabled` and `active`; the VLM's `/health` answers on :8005.
2. **The brain is pointed at it:** the brain's `/brain/health` reports
   `tier_local_vlm: true`.
3. **The brain's client gets an answer:** `brain/local_vlm.py`'s
   `LocalVLMClient`, with the URL and secret the stack runs with, asks one
   recorded frame and gets status `answered` within 3.0 s
   (`tier_local_timeout_s`).
4. **Memory:** with the VLM resident and the stack idle, `MemAvailable` is
   at least 1.0 GB (3.81's floor is 0.5 GB under load).
5. **The twin still works:** the deployed twin's `/brain/health` through
   the tunnel answers with the same `git_revision` as before.
