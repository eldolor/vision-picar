# 3.105 The bridge holds ready until nav2 can take a goal

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.105; index: [README.md](README.md).
Opened 2026-10-26 on the user's word, from 3.50.D4's open finding.*

**Why.** Found by 3.50.D4's AWS runs (2026-10-24; D4-b failed on it in both
houses): after a container start on a saved map, the bridge's `/health`
`slam.state` reads `ready` once the reload is judged (~1.7 s, 3.50.D3),
but nav2's lifecycle manager activates its nodes later (~13 s on the
laptop). In that window `navigate_to_pose` has a server, so the bridge's
`wait_for_server` passes and it answers 200 `pending`, and nav2 then
rejects the goal. **The facts, as read on `dev` 2026-10-26:** the bridge
records it `rejected` (`_on_goal_response`); `brain/explore.py` takes
`rejected` as terminal and not `succeeded`, so the goal counts in
`goals_failed` and its place is failed. A 503 from the bridge, instead, is
a retryable fault (`world/bridge_faults.py`) that explore waits on. So on
the car, the first goal after a start can fail a place that was reachable.
The D4 harness retries a rejected goal since its review; the brain does not.

**The change.** The bridge asks nav2's lifecycle manager
(`/lifecycle_manager_navigation/is_active`, `std_srvs/Trigger`) on a
timer, outside its lock, and caches the answer. Until it answers active,
`POST /goal` is refused 503 "nav2 is not active" before anything is sent
or cancelled, as a goal is while `slam.state` is not `ready`. `slam.state`
itself is unchanged: it is SLAM's state, and the map pose and `/slam/map`
need not wait for nav2. `/health` reports `nav2.active` and when it was
last asked.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. In process: while the lifecycle manager answers not active, has no
   service, or its call fails or is not answered, `POST /goal` is 503,
   retryable by `bridge_faults`, and nothing is sent to nav2 or cancelled.
   Red on `dev` first (`dev` answers 200 and sends).
2. In process: once it answers active, goals go as on `dev`; a later
   inactive answer (nav2 reset) holds goals again. `/health` shows
   `nav2.active`.
3. The bridge, goal, Stop, ROS-drive, reload and re-map suites unchanged
   (`test_review_376h1`, `test_map_persist`, `test_ros_goals`,
   `test_stop_cancels_goal`, `test_refused_goal_hold`, the wall linters).
4. Live (laptop, this branch's image; asked first if heavy): 5 container
   starts on a saved `scaled_house` map, a goal posted the moment
   `slam.state` reads `ready` and re-posted on each 503: **0 rejected**,
   every goal accepted and `succeeded`, and the 503 window recorded
   (bar: under 60 s each). On `dev`'s image the same posts are rejected
   at least once in 5 (the red side; D4-b's AWS runs saw 2 of 2).
5. Live: one explore mission from a container start on a saved map
   through the brain's HTTP API: `goals_failed` has no `rejected` goal
   and the mission does not end `failed`; 0 contacts.
