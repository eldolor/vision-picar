# 3.110 Goal facts 17 to 42: is there a step?

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.110; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user, 2026-10-26, after 3.103: "Was there a step change, or is
it possible to find out if there is a step change between 17 and 42, which
might be the sweet spot?", then "Start stage 1". Only two points on the way
from 17 facts (3.96's F-large) to 42 (3.101's F-rich) were ever trained the
same way, so a step, a smooth climb and a plateau cannot be told apart.
3.101's importance test (`evaluations/goal-learn-3101/run/importance.json`)
hints at a step early (`lidar_gain` -14 points of clone agreement when
shuffled, four more at -5 to -10, twenty under -2.5), but each drop was
measured with the other 41 facts present.

**Stage 1 (this section): a copying ladder, free, on the desktop.** Copying
the oracle (held-out clone agreement) is the most sensitive measure these
runs have; it is not the score (3.101: 35% -> 43% copying bought +0.010
reward), so a step here is a candidate for stage 2, not a result about
searches.

* **Rungs:** F-large's 17 facts, then 3.101's 25 new facts added one at a
  time, k = 0..25 (17 to 42 facts), in the order of their shuffled clone
  agreement drop, largest first (ties in listed order): `lidar_gain`,
  `since_new_ground`, `pool_size`, `time_used`, `gain_rank`, `path_rank`,
  `trail_near`, `room_seen`, `path_gap`, `unknown_4m`, `cluster_2m`,
  `trail_min`, `unknown_2m`, `cam_gain`, `known_share`, `room_hops`,
  `wall_clear`, `since_growth`, `rule_pick`, `detour`, `room_degree`,
  `nearest_other`, `room_dead_end`, `dominated`, `gain_per_m`. (The order
  comes from F-rich's own held-out choices; fixed here before any rung runs.)
* **Training, every rung alike:** F-large's network shape (S32x32,
  `w . f + MLP 32x32`), 3.96's clone (30 epochs, batch 256, lr 1e-3), 3.101's
  oracle sets rebuilt here by 3.101's harness, sliced to the rung's columns;
  **three seeds** (3961, 3962, 3963), so 78 clones. No PPO, no test houses.
* **Checked before the curve is read:** the rebuilt oracle sets give B's
  counts (37,064 training choices, 609 held out); rung 0 at seed 3961
  reproduces F-large's 34.7%, rung 1 B's S32x32 37.4%, rung 25 F-rich's
  43.0%, each within 0.5 point (reported either way; a miss is a difference
  in the code path, named before the curve is used).

**Criteria (written before any rung ran; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **A step** at rung k: the seeds' mean agreement at k is at least **3.0
   points** above rung k-1's, and every seed at k is above every seed at
   k-1. (609 held-out choices: one seed's agreement is +-2 points, so a
   smaller jump is not resolved.)
2. **The knee:** the smallest k whose mean is within **1.0 point** of the
   best rung's mean.
3. **Reported:** the curve (mean, min and max over seeds, all 26 rungs),
   every step by criterion 1, the knee, and the per-rung gain over rung 0.

**What follows from it (stage 2, not started; needs the user's word and
spot spending):** if criterion 1 finds a step, PPO and one test-house run
for the rung after the step, the knee and their neighbours, against F-large
by 3.103's paired test; if the curve is a smooth climb to a plateau with no
step, that is the answer and stage 2 is not proposed.
