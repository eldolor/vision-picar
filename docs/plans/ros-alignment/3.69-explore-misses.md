# 3.69 Explore's misses in the imported houses

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.69; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** 3.66 imported 1,000 ProcTHOR floor plans and ran `explore` over
the first 100 usable ones: found 84, bar 90, FAILED. None of the 16 misses
was an import fault. They fall into four classes of `explore` behaviour
(3.66's entry has the houses and the evidence):

* **A, 8 houses:** the target seen only while the camera was panned;
  `_sighting()` ignores a panned frame and nothing turned the body back.
* **B, 4 houses:** a good sighting, but the approach point the furniture
  left reachable is beyond the 0.40 m arrival radius, and nothing closes
  the last few centimetres.
* **C, 2 houses:** the lidar ranged the wrong surface at the sighting's
  bearing (the target at the frame's edge), so the approach went to the
  wrong place.
* **D, 2 houses:** never in any frame, though coverage counted the floor
  round it as seen. Not explained yet.

The user asked (2026-10-11) for a section that fixes them, judged on the
same 100 houses with the bar still at 90.

**The risk is tuning to these 16 houses**, the same fault 3.66 found in
four hand-drawn ones. So the fixes are judged on a second hundred houses
nobody has looked at, measured before any change, and the hand-drawn houses
keep every number they pin today.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

`FakeNav` sweeps as in 3.66 (`tests/procthor_sweep.py`, `max_steps` 200).
The **tuning set** is usable houses 0-99 (3.66's); the **held-out set** is
usable houses 100-199, measured once before any code change and not
examined house by house until the fixes are final.

1. **Found on the tuning set >= 90 of 100** (3.66's bar, unchanged).
2. **The held-out set does not get worse, and reaches the bar:** found
   after >= found before, and >= 90 of 100. No house found before may be
   lost unless its new miss is classified in this entry.
3. **Every sweep ends cleanly:** 100% of both sets with an outcome, no
   exception, under the tick cap.
4. **Progress, not just outcome** (section 7.4): on houses found both
   before and after, in both sets, the median goals sent per mission rises
   by no more than 10%.
5. **The hand-drawn houses keep their numbers:** the full suite passes,
   every existing explore test and pinned sweep number unchanged
   (`tests/test_explore*.py`, the house sweeps on `SLOW_FILES`).
6. **Each class is answered:** for A, B and C, a test that fails on `dev`
   and passes after (red first). For D, the cause is found and either
   fixed with a red-first test or recorded with the evidence.
7. **Through the real path, once:** one `explore` mission on the live
   stack (robot server + brain over HTTP + the ROS container, nav2) in
   `home_first_floor` ends with an outcome; the outcome is recorded.

If a bar fails, it is recorded as failed and pinned at the measured value,
as 3.66 did.
