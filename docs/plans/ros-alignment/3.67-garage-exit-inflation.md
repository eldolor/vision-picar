# 3.67 nav2's inflation against the garage exit

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.67; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The furnished-home tour fails goal 6 in every run since 3.57
fixed its point: the robot leaves goal 5 beside the car and stalls in the
~0.9 m passage between the garage's north wall and the car, where nav2's
path keeps it pressed to one side and the safety layer refuses its moves
(3.57, 0 of 3). nav2's `inflation_radius` is 0.12 m, barely past the
chassis' 15.1 cm circumscribed radius, so its paths hug solid things.
3.43 part b measured a candidate (`evaluations/slam-343/nav2-inflate.yaml`:
`inflation_radius` 0.12 -> 0.35 m, `cost_scaling_factor` 8 -> 5, both
costmaps): R6 6/6 with 36-43 cm from the nearest surface (19-25 cm today),
but not adopted, because explore's corner stalls (fixed since by 3.45)
remained. CLAUDE.md 3b records the user's choice: re-test it against the
garage exit. Nothing else changes: no code, only `nav2.yaml`, and only if
the criteria are met.

**Instrument (unchanged from 3.45, so the numbers compare).** Three fresh
furnished-home runs, `python -m tests.demo_slam_home 3 --tour 1
--explore-first 900 --nav evaluations/slam-343/nav2-inflate.yaml`, on
`vision-picar-ros:plan-3.67`, a tag of `:latest` (built 2026-10-07; dev
has changed only 3.47's `brain_view.py` diagnostics text in
`service/slam` since, which neither explore nor the tour reads). The tour
starts after 900 s of explore; goal 6 follows goal 5 in the garage.

**Criteria (written before measuring; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **The garage exit.** Goal 6 `succeeded` in **3 of 3** runs, ending
   <= 0.15 m from its point (3.57's bar). No run is replaced.
2. **Tour.** Each run reaches **>= 8 of 9** goals.
3. **No stalls.** In every run the longest stretch within 5 cm of one spot
   is **< 120 s**, explore and tour alike (`evaluations/slam-343/parked.py`,
   unchanged). Today: 99, 93, 88 s (3.45).
4. **Explore's progress holds.** Coverage at 900 s
   (`tests/demo_explore.coverage()`): the median of the three is
   **>= 0.83** (3.45's 0.883 less 0.05), and no run is below **0.68**
   (3.45's lowest, 0.730, less 0.05). Wider bands than one run's spread
   would make this a coin toss; narrower would fail on noise 3.45 itself
   showed (0.73-0.94).
5. **SLAM holds (3.40's bars):** `evaluations/slam-340/judge.py` passes in
   all three runs.
6. **R6 on the scaled house 6/6** with the candidate
   (`evaluations/slam-340/with_stack.py scaled_house '' -- python -m
   tests.demo_nav_goals`, with `--nav`), nearest surface reported.

**If 1-6 are all met:** the candidate becomes `nav2.yaml` (both costmaps),
the ROS engineering spec says so in the same commit, the full suite
passes, and `:latest` is rebuilt from `dev` after the merge (never from
this branch). **If any fails:** recorded as failed, `nav2.yaml` unchanged,
and the failure's location is read from the logs for the next step.
