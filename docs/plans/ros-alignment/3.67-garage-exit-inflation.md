# 3.67 nav2's inflation against the garage exit

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.67; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The furnished-home tour fails goal 6 in every run since 3.57
fixed its point: the robot leaves goal 5 beside the car and stalls in the
~0.9 m passage between the garage's north wall and the car, where nav2's
path keeps it pressed to one side and the safety layer refuses its moves
(3.57, 0 of 3). nav2's `inflation_radius` is 0.12 m, barely past the
chassis' 15.1 cm circumscribed radius, so its paths hug solid things.
3.43 part b measured a candidate (`evaluations/slam-343/nav2-inflate.yaml`:
`inflation_radius` 0.12 -> 0.35 m, `cost_scaling_factor` 8 -> 5, both
costmaps): R6 6/6 with 36-43 cm from the nearest surface (19-25 cm today),
but not adopted, because explore's corner stalls (fixed since by 3.45)
remained. CLAUDE.md 3b records the user's choice: re-test it against the
garage exit. Nothing else changes: no code, only `nav2.yaml`, and only if
the criteria are met.

**Instrument (unchanged from 3.45, so the numbers compare).** Three fresh
furnished-home runs, `python -m tests.demo_slam_home 3 --tour 1
--explore-first 900 --nav evaluations/slam-343/nav2-inflate.yaml`, on
`vision-picar-ros:plan-3.67`, a tag of `:latest` (built 2026-10-07; dev
has changed only 3.47's `brain_view.py` diagnostics text in
`service/slam` since, which neither explore nor the tour reads). The tour
starts after 900 s of explore; goal 6 follows goal 5 in the garage.

**Criteria (written before measuring; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

1. **The garage exit.** Goal 6 `succeeded` in **3 of 3** runs, ending
   <= 0.15 m from its point (3.57's bar). No run is replaced.
2. **Tour.** Each run reaches **>= 8 of 9** goals.
3. **No stalls.** In every run the longest stretch within 5 cm of one spot
   is **< 120 s**, explore and tour alike (`evaluations/slam-343/parked.py`,
   unchanged). Today: 99, 93, 88 s (3.45).
4. **Explore's progress holds.** Coverage at 900 s
   (`tests/demo_explore.coverage()`): the median of the three is
   **>= 0.83** (3.45's 0.883 less 0.05), and no run is below **0.68**
   (3.45's lowest, 0.730, less 0.05). Wider bands than one run's spread
   would make this a coin toss; narrower would fail on noise 3.45 itself
   showed (0.73-0.94).
5. **SLAM holds (3.40's bars):** `evaluations/slam-340/judge.py` passes in
   all three runs.
6. **R6 on the scaled house 6/6** with the candidate
   (`evaluations/slam-340/with_stack.py scaled_house '' -- python -m
   tests.demo_nav_goals`, with `--nav`), nearest surface reported.

**If 1-6 are all met:** the candidate becomes `nav2.yaml` (both costmaps),
the ROS engineering spec says so in the same commit, the full suite
passes, and `:latest` is rebuilt from `dev` after the merge (never from
this branch). **If any fails:** recorded as failed, `nav2.yaml` unchanged,
and the failure's location is read from the logs for the next step.

* **Criterion 1 -- FAILED, 0 of 3.** Criteria 2 (8/8/8), 3, 4 (median
  0.952) and 5 met; criterion 6 met: R6 6/6, 9-10 cm from each goal, nearest surface 37-42 cm, unreachable aborted in 17.9 s, tap 0.054 s, 0 safety refusals (`evaluations/slam-367/r6.out`). Goal 7 now succeeds
  after the failed goal 6 in all three runs (3.57's preemption effect did
  not appear).
* **Where it fails, the same in all three.** The robot leaves goal 5
  north, passes right under the garage's door to the hall at (12.97,
  8.85) m, and turns WEST along the passage between the garage's north
  wall and the car, ending in the north-west corner at (10.6, 8.7) m.
  There the robot server refuses nav2's forward moves 1,203-1,218 times
  (`safety_distance`) for the rest of the 120 s.
* **Why: nav2 plans through a wall it has never seen.** On the robot's
  own map at that moment (`maps.jsonl.gz`, version 1127), the garage's
  west wall is mapped along the passage but not behind the car: the
  stretch alongside the car is unknown. nav2's planner has
  `allow_unknown: true`, so the shortest plan runs south down the slot
  between the car and the west wall, then west through the unmapped
  stretch to the dining room. On ground truth the wall has no opening
  (`home_first_floor`, x = 33 ft, rows 26-48). The slot is 1 ft (0.305 m)
  wide: wider than nav2's footprint (0.231 m; `footprint_padding` 0.0 in the local costmap, nav2's default 0.01 in the global one),
  so it is plannable, but narrower than the safety layer's swept corridor
  (0.231 m plus `CREEP_MARGIN_CM` 3 cm a side and more with speed), so
  every forward move into it is refused. Inflation raises the slot's cost
  but cannot make it lethal; the detour through the hall door is long and
  the slot looks short.

### Amendment 1, before measuring candidate B (2026-10-10)

Two things make the slot attractive, and only one is in the planner's
model of the robot: its footprint is the chassis alone, while the
safety layer, which has the last word, needs a side margin. A passage
nav2 can plan but the robot server cannot let it drive is a wedge by
construction. **Candidate B:** candidate A plus `footprint_padding:
0.05` in both costmaps (`evaluations/slam-367/nav2-inflate-pad.yaml`):
the creep corridor's 3 cm a side, plus half a 5 cm map cell for where
a mapped wall really is. A passage then needs 0.331 m, which makes the
1-ft slot lethal; the house's 0.9 m doors are unaffected.
`allow_unknown` is left as it is: the tour maps as it goes, and
frontier goals sit at the edge of the unknown.

Candidate B is measured on criteria 1-6 unchanged, in three fresh runs
and R6. Criterion 4's coverage bars stay those of 3.45's runs, not
candidate A's, so B is not judged against a number this section chose
after seeing it.

### Amendment 2, before measuring candidate C (2026-10-11)

Candidate A's failure needed two things: a slot the safety layer will
not drive, and a plan that ran through **unmapped** wall behind it. B
attacked the first and broke the robot elsewhere. **Candidate C attacks
the second:** candidate A with the planner's `allow_unknown: false`
(`evaluations/slam-367/nav2-inflate-known.yaml`; no padding). nav2 then
plans only through cells the map has seen free. Explore is unaffected by
construction: `brain/frontier.py` already sends only goals it reached by
a search over known free cells, so every frontier goal has a known path.
The tour runs after 900 s of explore on a map with coverage near 0.95.
The risk, which the criteria catch: a goal whose only route crosses a
cell the map still calls unknown becomes unplannable.

Candidate C is measured on criteria 1-6 unchanged, three fresh runs and
R6, with criterion 4's bars still 3.45's.
