of `home_first_floor` (3.70 M5) and 11 of 13 with 3.83's fix, which was not
adopted (the user, 2026-10-16). 3.83's runs named two more causes:

* **Pantry: an explore livelock, not a wedge.** From 990 s to the end
  explore stood about 0.9 m from the sighting, answering "already facing
  it" with no goal live (`_send_approach`'s "at the target; looking" and
  `FACE`'s "already facing it" in `brain/explore.py`). It is in code 3.83

* **Breakfast: pinned in a pocket under the dining table.** From about
  700 s the robot sat at (7.89, 9.12): REVERSE refused (rear 1.2 cm),
  LEFT refused (a corner within 1.0 cm), only RIGHT about 54 deg, and nav2
  turned it back. A keepout keeps the planner out of a place; it cannot
  free a robot already in one. 3.83 placed its one mark there only after
  the robot was in.

3.83's code (refusal keepout, mark once per spot, the 20 s stall cut and
its 13 review fixes) is on branch `plan/3.83-dining-room-trap` and is the
starting point here.

**Plan, in order.**

1. **Diagnose the pantry loop, then fix it red-first.** Reproduce it in

**Step 1's diagnosis (2026-10-16, reproduced in process from 3.83's
pantry run).** Not a hang: the runner logs a wait only when its text
changes, and explore answered "already facing it" on every tick for
1,145 s. The robot stood at (12.93, 7.32), outside the pantry door, 0.82 m
from the backpack at (12.15, 7.05). The **detector** reports it (bearing
-3 deg): `GridWorld._detections` samples the object's extent
(`renderer.visible_bearing`), so a target half behind a jamb is seen. The
sim's **found** test does not: `frame_description()`'s `objects_visible`
casts one ray to the object's CENTRE (`renderer._visible_objects`), and
that ray stops at 1.2 cells in the wall cell (41, 24), the pantry's door
jamb. The loop itself: the approach just before (to (12.88, 7.29))
was refused `preempted` -- the brain's own FACE turn still held driving
authority -- but `_send_approach` had already recorded it as made
(`self._approach`). Every later frame's sighting of the same spot then
counted as already approached, so none was re-sent, and
`_back_to_sighting` (the robot within `APPROACH_FAR_M` + 0.1 of that
frame's estimate) only faced it again -- "already facing it" -- from
outside the door, where the line to the target is not clear. **Fixed:**
an approach counts as made only once nav2 took the goal (red-first,
`test_a_refused_approach_is_tried_again_not_given_up_for_a_look`). On
the car the same holds for any target half behind a door frame: seeing
part of it is not arriving at it.

**Step 2's diagnosis (2026-10-16, 3.83's breakfast run,
`/tmp/vision-picar-3.83/run-20261015-213352`).** At 761 s explore sent a
frontier goal to (8.63, 10.23) -- under the dining table, M5's trap point
(8.66, 10.26). It stalled at (7.95, 9.28) (812 s). The escape's REVERSE
waited on the goal's hold, then stopped after 0.4 cm; its **CREEP
backwards** (`_creep_ends`: the roomier end, at `CREEP_SPEED` 5, which
3.44's speed-dependent stop lets go closer than 20 cm) carried the robot
0.16 m to (7.89, 9.12), leaving 1.3 cm behind it. From there REVERSE is
refused (rear 1.3 cm < 20 cm), LEFT is refused (a corner within 1.0 cm)
and RIGHT stops after about 54 deg: pinned for the remaining 1,000 s. The
same trap frontier was sent again at 825, 899 and later -- the first stall
is forgiven as a wedge (`_goal_ended`), and failures land on a place only
after a second.

**Step 2's fix and its criteria (written before building it):**

* **(a) An escape tries the open end.** When the escape's REVERSE is
  refused and the front is open (`forward_clearance()` above
  `min_distance_cm`), it drives FORWARD once, guarded as any verb, before
  the creep and the turn. In the breakfast pocket the rear was 1.3 cm and
  the front about 30 cm (14.8 cm before the creep's 0.166 m), and the
  escape (REVERSE, CREEP, OPEN) never tried ahead; nav2's goals then
  turned left into a corner the pivot guard refused 362 times.
  *(Amended before building, 2026-10-16: the first draft limited how far
  a creep goes; a creep verb is one whole 0.30 m move that the safety
  layer stops at its creep margin -- 3.44's design -- so the brain cannot
  ask for a shorter one.)*
* **(b) A stall fails its goal's place at once.** A `stalled` goal is
  held against the place it was going to (one retry failure), so a trap
  frontier is not sent three times; a goal that never left (`moved` <
  `STUCK_MOVED_M`) keeps 3.38's first-wedge forgiveness.
* Red-first tests: an escape with REVERSE refused and the front open
  drives FORWARD; with the front closed it creeps and turns as before; a
  stalled goal's place counts one failure; a never-left goal's does not.
* Judged by criteria 1-7 below with steps 1 and 3.
