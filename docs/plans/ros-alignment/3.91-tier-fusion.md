# 3.91 Tier answers over time: disagreement, freshness and the approach (from the 2026-10-21 phone walk)

*`PLAN-ros-alignment.md` section 3.91; index: [README.md](README.md).*

**Why.** The user's first Drive-via-brain walk under 3.90's stream
(2026-10-21, `red-backpack-gpt-6-astra-20261021-165427`, tiered, GPT-6
Astra, 222 frames, all recorded) looped FORWARD / REVERSE about nine times
in 75 s and never ended. The walk's own rows say why:

* **Perception is blind at close range.** YOLOE + CLIP saw the backpack at
  P 0.80-0.98, bearing within 5 degrees of centre, then lost it on the
  first frame where it filled the picture (#57, #60, #135, #139, #205,
  #209): no outline, no proposal.
* **A missed frame falls to the held cloud goal, and the held goal is an
  obstacle answer.** The ladder in `TieredVision._local_scene()` is this
  frame's sighting, then dead-reckoning (needs odometry and
  `tier_hold_bearing`, so never on a phone), then an uncertain candidate,
  then `_held_direction()`. That is the cloud's **`safest_direction`**, an
  obstacle field, and CLAUDE.md 3c says the vision model's obstacle fields
  are uncalibrated. A frame full of backpack reads "blocked ahead":
  REVERSE, held for the rest of the walk.
* **The tiers disagreed and nothing resolved it.** All 10 cloud answers
  that landed were `no_claim` (the target not visible), 8 of them asked by
  `candidate_sighting` on frames perception had just called DETECTED. Each
  new local sighting raised the same question again: 11 paid calls on one
  object. The backpack is taupe; the target was "red backpack". CLIP does
  not weigh colour much, so the cloud may have been right.
* **Arrival needs perception to see the target centred at under 0.40 m**
  (`brain/arrival.py`). Where perception goes blind is unmeasured on any
  camera. If it is above 0.40 m, the Rover can never arrive and loops the
  same way.

**What cannot be told from the walk.** Rows record the decision on each
frame, not the cloud's answers: whether REVERSE meant "too close" or "not
the target" is lost (the vision Lambda logs only timings). Step A records
them; nothing is redesigned before B's numbers.

**Out of scope.** The tier split itself (perception every frame, the cloud
on triggers): the walk shows it working where it should -- perception
steady at mid range, 11 calls in 75 s. Colour in the CLIP gate is noted
for B, not changed here. The local VLM tier (3.80) stays off by default;
C may use it only if B shows it is needed.

**Cost.** A and B make no Bedrock call. Replaying the cloud over this
walk's frames (about 11 calls, about $0.35, an estimate) is asked first
and is not needed for A or B. C's live phone walk is the user's tiered
walk; its calls are counted, not bought by a harness.

**Criteria (written before building; CLAUDE.md section 7):**

*A. Record what the cloud said (free).*

1. **Every landed answer, once.** Each cloud answer that lands during a
   recorded walk appears in `walk.jsonl` exactly once, as `cloud_answer` on
   the row of the frame whose tick applied it: the trigger, the robot seq
   it was asked on, the verdict, `action`, `safest_direction`,
   `target_visible`, `target_direction`, `reasoning` (cut to 500
   characters) and `model_id`. A call that failed appears once as
   `cloud_error`. Tested with a stub cloud: N answers give N rows, 0
   missing, 0 doubled, red first.
2. **Read-only.** No decision changes: the tiered sweeps pinned in the
   suite are unchanged, and the stub-cloud tests' actions are identical.

*B. Measure (free; descriptive, so no bar, but each number is recorded).*

3. **The blind spot on this walk.** Perception replayed offline over all
   222 frames on the Jetson's GPU (as live): the share of frames DETECTED,
   and for each run of absent frames that follows a centred detection,
   whether the frame is filled by the target (labelled by eye, recorded
   per frame).
4. **Disagreement.** On walks recorded after A: of the cloud calls asked
   on a frame perception called DETECTED, the share the cloud answered
   "not visible". This walk, from its counters: at least 7 of 8.
5. **Range in metres** (needs one short walk by the user: the phone at rig
   height, about 12 cm, the target at taped 1.0, 0.7, 0.5, 0.4 and 0.3 m,
   about 5 s still at each). The share of frames DETECTED at each distance.
   **If under 80% at 0.40 m, arrival cannot rest on perception at that
   range**, and C must say what ends an approach. The phone is a proxy for
   the Rover's camera; the Rover repeats it on arrival.

*C. Combine answers over time (built only after B; criteria fixed now).*

6. **No loop.** This walk's frames replayed through `TieredVision` with a
   stub cloud answering as this walk's cloud did (not visible,
   `safest_direction` REVERSE): **at most 1 FORWARD -> REVERSE reversal**
   per approach (this walk: about 9).
7. **One question per object.** On the same replay, **at most 4 cloud
   calls** (this walk: 11): a cloud "not visible" on a candidate suppresses
   asking again about that candidate until the robot has moved or a set
   time has passed (C names which, from B).
8. **No regression.** The tiered sweeps pinned in the suite (3.80.G's 21
   houses, 3.80.O's blind perception at paces 1 and 6) find at least as
   many as pinned, with 0 contacts.
9. **Live.** One Drive-via-brain walk by the user on the deployed twin:
   at most 1 reversal per approach, and the walk's rows (A) say why every
   held direction was held.

## Results

**A (2026-10-21), merged to `dev` at `00f5647c`.** Built: each landed
cloud answer, and each failed call, is filed once as `cloud_answers` on the
decision row of the first new frame decided after it landed (`asked_seq`
names the frame it was asked on), or in `meta.json`
`cloud_answers_after_last_frame` if it landed after the last decision.
Criterion 1: 20 tests in `tests/test_tier_cloud_answers.py`, red on the
code each review round replaced, green after. Criterion 2: no decision
changes (both reviewers checked against `origin/dev` in round 4); the gate
passed (2,984). Four review rounds, both reviewers each round, changed the
design: the policy only queues answers and the runner takes them (a scene
taken into could be thrown away); the last take notes a landed call without
applying it (it runs on the Stop's thread before the motors stop); a
failure keeps its type and HTTP status, never its text (walk rows go to
S3); a repeated frame keeps its first answers. Known limits (recordings
spec): the arrival confirmation is not recorded; a tick that took a landed
call but had not yet noted it when the Stop's take ran loses that note; a
recording stopped before its mission misses the leftovers.

**B, criterion 3 (2026-10-21; `evaluations/tier-fusion-391/`,
`replay_perception.py`, `B3-replay-jetson.json`).** YOLOE + CLIP replayed
over all 222 frames on the Jetson's GPU: the replay matches the live
status on all 96 frames perceived live. Before the first sighting (seq 11)
the target is out of view. After it, **73 of 211 frames (35%) are lost,
every one at close range**, in two kinds:

* **32 with no box at all** (YOLOE proposes nothing; by eye the frame is
  backpack fabric, e.g. #57, #60, #135, #139, #205, #209).
* **41 with one box that is the whole frame** (median 98% of its area),
  which CLIP scores under the 0.8 gate (24 of them at P 0.5-0.8): the crop
  is the picture, not the object.

Even the frames DETECTED are close: the box covers a median 85% of the
frame (p10 56%, p90 95%), width 90%. So this walk was an approach that
stayed inside the range where perception is unreliable; nothing in it
says where that range begins in metres (criterion 5).

**Found on the way, open.** Perception took a median **124 ms** a frame
replayed alone and **413 ms** live (p90 440) on the same board and frames:
3.3x slower inside the running stack. 3.80.P measured a p90 of 171 ms at
pace 1. Not 3.91's question; recorded for 3.90's B and C, whose rates rest
on it.
