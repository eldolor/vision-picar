## 1. The headline: order the Pi + Hailo-8L. The reactive tier is 45%.

P9 measured YOLO-World + CLIP at **72%** against the shipped YOLO11s +
CLIP's **45%** (fp32, at the shipped `P>=0.8` gate, 11 walks). P10 then
compiled YOLO-World to a Hailo-8L. It looked like a 72% tier for $70.

**It is not, and the reason is quantization.** P13 measured five INT8
configurations; the best reaches **5% where fp32 reaches 34%** on the
target-class score, and on the fairer proposal-agreement metric
YOLO-World retains **70%** of fp32's confident boxes where **YOLO11s
retains 99%** -- and 93% at `LOW_CONFIDENCE` 0.05, which is where the
pipeline actually operates for an out-of-vocabulary target.

So the part runs the **shipped** pipeline essentially losslessly and the
open-vocabulary one badly. **45% is what you can buy.**

Why, mechanically: YOLO-World's head is a **cosine similarity over a
512-d embedding**, and direction is exactly what a per-tensor INT8 scale
scrambles. YOLO11s has no such stage. That also explains why promoting
three convs to 16-bit barely helped -- the damage accumulates along the
whole embedding path.

## 4. Things that were wrong, so they are not re-learned

- **A trivial-input control should be the FIRST test, not the fifth.** Two
  conclusions about the 10H were written down before one -- "the missing
  system packages are the root cause" and "the vendor's distribution is
  broken" -- and both were wrong. A 4-node conv ONNX settled it in
  minutes.
- **The DFC reports a bad precision MODE as a problem with the LAYER.**
  Three rounds went after layer selection while the mode string was wrong
  (`a16_w8` does not exist; the names have three parts).
- **Read the warnings.** `Reducing optimization level to 0` was printed on
  every run for hours and cost a whole result.
- **Do not edit a bash script while a background job is executing it** --
  bash re-reads as it goes.
- **SSM reaps a `nohup`'d process group.** Two runs died at exactly frame
  201 of 365. `setsid` fixes it.
- **One heavy DFC job per box.** Two on a 15GB host OOM-killed each other
  and cost **$12 overnight for nothing**. `ec2.sh` now arms a
  self-shutdown backstop; use it.
- **`labels.json`'s `description` IS the target string the pipeline is
  asked for**, not a human note. Writing a long sentence there would have
  silently confounded P9.
